**Q1.** A particle of mass m is projected with velocity v at an angle of 45° with the horizontal. When the particle is at its maximum height, its angular momentum about the point of projection is: [Medium]
*Topic: Physics*

A) mv³ / (4√2 g)
B) mv³ / (2√2 g)
C) mv³ / (√2 g)
D) Zero

> **Correct Answer:** A

> **Explanation:**
> **Step 1: Variables at Maximum Height (H)**
> At max height, vertical velocity is 0. Horizontal velocity vx = v cos 45° = v/√2.
> Position vector r has coordinates (R/2, H).
> H = v² sin²45° / 2g = v² / 4g.
> Range R = v² sin 90° / g = v² / g. So x = R/2 = v² / 2g.
> **Step 2: Angular Momentum Def**
> L = r × p. Here L = m (r × v).
> Position r = (v²/2g) i + (v²/4g) j.
> Velocity at top v_top = (v/√2) i.
> L = m [ (v²/2g)i + (v²/4g)j ] × [ (v/√2)i ].
> L = m (v²/4g)(v/√2) (j × i) = m (v³/4√2g) (-k).
> **Conclusion:** Magnitude is mv³ / (4√2 g).

**Q2.** Two solid spheres A and B of same material have radii r and 2r respectively. They are at the same temperature. The ratio of their rates of cooling (dT/dt) in the same surroundings is: [Easy]
*Topic: Physics*

A) 2 : 1
B) 1 : 1
C) 1 : 2
D) 4 : 1

> **Correct Answer:** A

> **Explanation:**
> **Step 1: Rate of Cooling Formula**
> Rate of cooling R = dT/dt = (eAσ(T⁴ - T₀⁴)) / ms.
> Since material and temp are same, R ∝ A/m.
> **Step 2: Geometry Relations**
> Area A ∝ r². Mass m ∝ Volume ∝ r³.
> So R ∝ r² / r³ = 1/r.
> **Step 3: Ratio**
> R_A / R_B = r_B / r_A = 2r / r = 2 : 1.
> **Conclusion:** The smaller sphere cools twice as fast.

1. Molecular Nature & Gas Laws

Behaviour of Gases

At very low pressures and high temperatures, real gases behave like Ideal Gases. The state of an ideal gas is defined by the Ideal Gas Equation.

PV = μRT = NkBT
Ideal Gas Equation (μ: moles, N: molecules, R: Gas const, kB: Boltzmann const).

Avogadro's Hypothesis

Equal volumes of all ideal gases at the same temperature and pressure contain the same number of molecules (NA = 6.022 × 1023).

2. Kinetic Theory of Ideal Gas

Core Assumptions of KTG

  • Identical Spheres: Gas consists of large number of identical, perfectly elastic, point-sized molecules.
  • Random Motion: Molecules move randomly in all directions.
  • Collisions: Collisions between molecules and walls are perfectly elastic. Total energy remains constant.
  • Time of Collision: The time spent during collision is negligible compared to the time between collisions.
P = (1/3) ρ vrms2 = (1/3) (N/V) m vrms2
Pressure exert by an ideal gas. Note: P depends on ρ and vrms.

Kinetic Interpretation of Temperature

Temperature is a measure of the average translational kinetic energy of molecules. It is NOT depending on the nature of gas.

Eavg = (3/2) kBT

3. Molecular Speeds

🚀

RMS Speed (vrms)

√(3RT/M)

Tap to reveal

vrms = √(3kBT/m)
Used in pressure and energy calculations. Highest of the three speeds.

📊

Avg Speed (vavg)

√(8RT/πM)

Tap to reveal

vavg = √(8kBT/πm)
Arithmetic mean of all speeds. vrms ≈ 1.08 vavg.

🎯

Most Probable (vmp)

√(2RT/M)

Tap to reveal

vmp = √(2kBT/m)
Speed possessed by the maximum number of molecules. Lowest speed.

💡

Speed Ratio Order

vrms > vavg > vmp

Mnemonic: RAM (RMS > Average > Most Probable)

4. Law of Equipartition of Energy

Total energy is equally distributed among all degrees of freedom (f). Energy per degree of freedom per molecule = (1/2) kBT.

Degrees of Freedom (f) Summary

Gas Type Example Translational Rotational Total (f)
Monatomic He, Ar 3 0 3
Diatomic H2, O2 3 2 5
Polyatomic H2O, CH4 3 3 6

5. Specific Heat Capacity

Cv = (f/2)R
Cp = (1 + f/2)R
γ = Cp / Cv = 1 + 2/f
Molar Heat Capacities in terms of Degrees of Freedom.

6. Mean Free Path (λ)

The average distance travelled by a molecule between two successive collisions.

λ = 1 / (√2 π d2 n)
d: molecular diameter, n: number density (N/V).

Shortcut Dependencies

  • λ ∝ 1/P (at constant Temp)
  • λ ∝ T (at constant Pressure)
  • λ ∝ 1/d2

The "Mixture" Trick

fmix = (n1f1 + n2f2) / (n1 + n2)

Thermal Properties of Matter

Physics Unit 11
25 min read
IAT Advanced
Hot Topic

1. Core Concept

Heat vs. Temp

Heat: Energy in transit (Joules).
Temperature: Average kinetic energy of molecules (Kelvin).

PV = μRT
Ideal Gas Equation (T must be in Kelvin). P ∝ T at constant V; V ∝ T at constant P.

Specific Heat (s) vs. Heat Capacity (S)

Feature Specific Heat (s) Heat Capacity (S or C)
Definition Heat for 1kg mass Heat for entire body
Formula s = Q / (mΔT) S = Q / ΔT = ms
Property Intensive (Material property) Extensive (Depends on mass)

2. Key Formulas

📏

Linear Expansion (α)

ΔL / (L0ΔT)

Tap to reveal

α = Coefficient of Linear Expansion.
Fractional change in length per unit temperature rise.

🔳

Area Expansion (β)

Relation with α?

Tap to reveal

β = 2α
Coefficient of Superficial Expansion. Used for areas of plates and sheets.

🧊

Volume Expansion (γ)

Relation with α?

Tap to reveal

γ = 3α
Coefficient of Cubical Expansion. Note: Liquids only have γ.

Heat & Phase Change:

Temp change: Q = msΔT (s = Specific heat).

Phase change: Q = mL (L = Latent heat).

Modes of Heat Transfer:

⚛️

Conduction

Medium required?

Tap to reveal

Yes. Transfer via molecular vibrations and free electrons. Fastest in solids (metals).

🌪️

Convection

What moves?

Tap to reveal

Actual mass moves. Hot fluid rises, cold fluid sinks. Gravity is required!

☀️

Radiation

Speed?

Tap to reveal

Speed of Light (c). No medium required. T4 dependence (Stefan's Law).

H = dQ/dt = KAΔT / L
Conduction (K = Thermal Conductivity).
P = eσAT4
Radiation (Stefan-Boltzmann: σ ≈ 5.67 × 10-8).
dθ/dt = -K(θ - θsurr)
Newton’s Law of Cooling (Valid for small Δθ).

3. Elite Shortcuts & Memory Tricks

Thermal Resistance (Ohm's Law Analogy):

Treat Heat Current (H) as Current, Temp Diff (ΔT) as Voltage, and Thermal Resistance (Rth = L/KA) as Resistance.

  • Series: Req = R1+R2
  • Parallel: 1/Req = 1/R1+1/R2

Pendulum Clock Error

If the temperature increases by ΔT, the time lost/gained by a pendulum clock in a day (86400 seconds) is:

Δt = (1/2) α ΔT × 86400

Pro Tip: Temp rises &implies; expansion &implies; T increases &implies; clock runs SLOW (loses time).

Water's Density Anomaly:

Densest at 4°C. Expands when cooled 4°C to 0°C. (Ice floats, lakes freeze from top down).

4. Conceptual Insights

Thermal Stress:

Thermal stress occurs ONLY if expansion is physically restricted. If clamped, Force = YAαΔT. (Fact: Independent of length!).

Radiation Dominance:

Radiation is the only mode of transfer in a vacuum. Note P ∝ T4. Doubling temp increases power 16x.

Wien’s Law (λmaxT = b):

Connects color to temperature. Blue star = Hotter; Red star = Cooler.

5. Common Traps & Mistakes

⚠️

The Phase Change Trap:

Q=msΔT and Q=mL are mutually exclusive steps. Use a temp-heat graph to ensure you don't combine them.

⚠️

The Specific Heat of Gases Trap:

For gases, Cp - Cv = R. Always verify if the process is isobaric (Cp) or isochoric (Cv).

⚠️

Newton’s Law Validity:

Cooling formula is an approximation, accurate only for small Δθ (< 20-30°C).

6. Example Applications

Interface Temperature (Series Rods):

For two rods in series, T = (THR2 + TLR1) / (R1+R2) (Weighted average).

Mixing Ice and Water:

Always verify if the heat released by the hot body is sufficient to melt the ice. If not, final equilibrium is 0°C.

7. IAT Exam Focus Points

Degrees of Freedom (f)

  • Monoatomic (He): f=3.
  • Diatomic (H2): f=5 (at normal temps).
  • γ = Cp/Cv = 1 + 2/f.

Thermal Expansion of Holes:

If a hole is cut in a metal plate, the hole expands as if it were a solid piece of the same metal. It does NOT shrink.

U-Tube Manometers & Temperature:

Expansion affects density (ρ' = ρ0/(1+γΔT)). Heat causes columns to rise for the same mass.

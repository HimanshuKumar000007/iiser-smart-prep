1. Core Concept

Wave Classification

Waves are classified based on the direction of particle displacement relative to wave propagation.

Property Transverse Waves Longitudinal Waves
Displacement Perpendicular to propagation Parallel to propagation
Medium Solids & String surfaces Solids, Liquids, and Gases
Pressure No pressure changes Pressure vs. Density variations
Polarization Can be polarized Cannot be polarized

Mechanical Waves

Propagation of a disturbance through a medium. Particles oscillate about equilibrium, but energy travels.

  • Transverse: Particles move ⊥ to wave velocity (e.g., strings).
  • Longitudinal: Particles move ∥ to wave velocity (e.g., sound).

2. Progressive Wave Equation

y(x,t) = A sin(kx ∓ ωt + φ)
Generalized Wave Equation. k = 2π/λ (Wave Number), ω = 2πf (Angular Frequency).
vp = -vwave × (slope)
Particle Velocity (vp) vs Wave Velocity (vwave). Slope is ∂y/∂x.

Phase & Path Difference

Δφ = (2π/λ) Δx
Phase difference (Δφ) related to path difference (Δx).

3. Speed of Travelling Waves

Wave Speed Summary Grid

Medium Wave Type Speed Formula (v)
Stretched String Transverse √(T/μ)
Solid Rod Longitudinal √(Y/ρ)
Fluid/Gas Longitudinal √(B/ρ)
Gas (Laplace) Longitudinal √(γP/ρ)
v = fλ = ω/k
Universal Wave Speed Relation. Dependent ONLY on the medium properties.

4. Principle of Superposition

When two or more waves overlap, the resultant displacement is the algebraic sum of individual displacements:

yres = y1 + y2 + ... + yn

6. Beats

Beat Frequency

Periodic variations in intensity heard when two sound waves of slightly different frequencies overlap.

fbeats = |f1 - f2|
Number of beats per second. For human ear to distinguish, fbeats ≤ 10 Hz.

Tuning Fork Logic

  • Waxing (Adding mass): f decreases.
  • Filing (Removing mass): f increases.

7. Conceptual Insights

Wave Speed Nuances

Wave speed (v) is a property of the medium. Source changes (f, A) do not affect v, but they do change λ via v = fλ.

Energy Transport

Mechanical waves transport energy and momentum, but NOT medium particles. Average Power Pavg ∝ A2ω2.

8. Common Mistakes & Traps

Directional Sign Trap

For y = A sin(kx ∓ ωt):
(kx - ωt) &implies; Moves in +x direction.
(kx + ωt) &implies; Moves in -x direction.

Newton-Laplace Correction

Newton assumed Isothermal (v = √(P/ρ)).
Laplace corrected it to Adiabatic (v = √(γP/ρ)) because compressions/rarefactions are too fast for heat exchange.

9. IAT Exam Focus Points

Standing Waves Phasing

All particles in a single loop (between two nodes) oscillate in the same phase. Particles in adjacent loops are 180° (π) out of phase.

Intensity Variations

For a point source: I ∝ 1/r2 (Spherical wave).
For a line source: I ∝ 1/r (Cylindrical wave).

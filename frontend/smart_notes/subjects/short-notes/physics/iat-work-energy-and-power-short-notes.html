Work, Energy, Power and Collisions

Physics Unit 5
25 min read
IAT Advanced
Hot Topic

1. Core Concept

The Master Equation (Work-Energy Theorem - WET)

The total work done by ALL forces (conservative, non-conservative, internal, external) is exactly equal to the change in Kinetic Energy.

Wnet = ΔK

Types of Forces & Energy

  • Conservative Forces: Work done is independent of the path taken and zero in a closed loop (e.g., Gravity, Springs, Electrostatic).
  • Non-Conservative Forces: Work done depends on the path (e.g., Friction, Viscous drag). They dissipate mechanical energy.
  • Potential Energy (U): Defined strictly for conservative forces. It is the energy of configuration.

Conservation of Mechanical Energy

If Wnon-conservative = 0 and Wexternal = 0, then Total Mechanical Energy (E = K + U) is strictly constant:

Ki + Ui = Kf + Uf

Power (P):

Rate of doing work.

  • Average Power: Pavg = ΔW / Δt
  • Instantaneous Power: Pinst = F · v

Collisions:

e = (Velocity of Separation) / (Velocity of Approach)
Coefficient of Restitution e (Strictly evaluated along the Line of Impact).

3. Elite Shortcuts & Memory Tricks

The Bullet & Plank Trick:

If a bullet loses 1/nth of its initial velocity while passing through one plank, the total number of identical planks required to stop it completely is:

N = n2 / (2n - 1)

Hanging Chain Pull-up:

A uniform chain of mass M, length L has 1/nth of its length hanging off a table. The work done to pull it slowly onto the table is:

W = MgL / (2n2)

Constant Power Kinematics:

If an engine delivers constant power P to a particle starting from rest (u=0):

  • Velocity grows as: v ∝ t1/2
  • Displacement grows as: s ∝ t3/2

1D Elastic Collision (e=1)

If m1 = m2, their velocities simply interchange after the collision!

Bouncing Ball (Inelastic Series):

Ball dropped from height h0 on a floor with restitution e:

  • Height after nth bounce: hn = e2n h0
  • Total distance covered before stopping: D = h0(1 + e2) / (1 - e2)

Vertical Circular Motion (VCM) Summary

Conservation of Energy analysis for a mass on a string (Length R):

  • To Complete Circle: vbottom ≥ √(5gR)
  • To Reach Top (v=0): vbottom ≥ √(4gR)
  • Oscillates (stays in lower half): vbottom ≤ √(2gR)

Trick: Tension difference Tbottom - Ttop = 6mg (Always!)

Work by Gravity on Projectile

Work done by gravity from point (x₁, y₁) to (x₂, y₂) is path-independent:

W = -mg(y2 - y1)

Zero work for a full flight back to the same horizontal level!

4. Conceptual Insights & Stability

To analyze stability, find points where net force is zero: F = -dU/dx = 0.

💎

Stable Equilibrium

Nature of restoring force?

Tap to reveal

d2U/dx2 > 0
(Potential Minima ∪). If displaced, force pushes it back to mean position. Executes SHM.

⚠️

Unstable Equilibrium

What happens if displaced?

Tap to reveal

d2U/dx2 < 0
(Potential Maxima ∩). If displaced, force pulls it further away. Never returns.

⚖️

Neutral Equilibrium

Work done on displacement?

Tap to reveal

d2U/dx2 = 0
(Flat Surface). Particle stays in equilibrium at its new position. Force remains zero.

5. Common Traps & Mistakes

⚠️

The Spring Work Sign Trap:

  • Work done BY the spring when stretched from 0 to x: W = -(1/2)kx2
  • Work done BY an external agent slowly stretching it: W = +(1/2)kx2
⚠️

Work is Frame-Dependent:

Since displacement (s) depends on the observer's frame of reference, Work and Kinetic Energy are different for different observers. However, the Work-Energy Theorem (W = ΔK) holds true in ALL inertial frames!

⚠️

Internal Forces CAN Do Work:

Don't assume Winternal = 0. In a bomb explosion, internal chemical forces do positive work to increase the system's kinetic energy.

⚠️

Applying e wrongly in 2D Collisions:

In oblique collisions, the e formula ONLY applies to velocity components along the common normal (Line of Impact). Tangential velocity components remain unchanged!

6. Example Applications

Work Done by Variable Friction:

Pushing a block horizontally over a rough, curved, hilly surface from x = 0 to x = d.

Insight: You don't need the equation of the curve! Since ds = dx / cos θ and N = mg cos θ, the work done by kinetic friction is simply:
W = -∫μk N ds = -∫μk mg dx = -μk mg d. (Treat it like a flat horizontal surface).

Engine Pumping Water:

A pump lifts water (mass m) from depth h and ejects it from a pipe with velocity v in time t.

Insight: The pump does two things: lifts the water AND gives it kinetic energy. Total Work = mgh + (1/2)mv2.
Power P = (m/t) *[gh + v2/2].

7. IAT Exam Focus Points

Partial Derivatives (F from U) - Extremely high frequency:

Given U(x,y) = xy2. Find force at (1, 2).

Method:
Fx = -∂U/∂x = -y2.
Fy = -∂U/∂y = -2xy.
Plug in (1, 2) &implies; F = -4 î - 4 ĵ.

Energy Conservation with Springs & Blocks

"Maximum compression" occurs when both blocks (or block and spring) have the same velocity. Use Momentum Conservation to find this common velocity, then Energy Conservation to find the spring compression.

Turning Points in Potential Graphs:

Given a graph of U(x) and a Total Energy line E. The kinetic energy is K = E - U(x).
Since K cannot be negative, the particle is restricted to regions where E ≥ U.
The points where E = U are the turning points where the particle momentarily stops and reverses direction.

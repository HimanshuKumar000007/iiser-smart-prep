Laws of Motion (Dynamics, Friction & Circular Motion)

30 min read

Core Mechanics

1. Core Concept

Newton’s 1st Law (Inertia)

A body maintains its state of rest/uniform motion unless a net external force acts. Defines Inertial Frames (frames with zero acceleration).

Inertial vs Non-Inertial Frames

  • Inertial: aframe = 0 (Ground, or constant velocity). Newton's laws valid directly.
  • Non-Inertial: aframe ≠ 0 (Accelerating bus, lift). Use Pseudo Force to apply Newton's laws.

Newton’s 2nd Law (The Real Law)

Force equals the rate of change of momentum:

Fnet = dp/dt

If mass is constant, Fnet = ma.

Newton’s 3rd Law (Action-Reaction)

Forces always occur in pairs. Action and reaction are equal, opposite, and act simultaneously on different bodies. They never cancel each other because they act on different objects.

3. Important Results & Shortcuts (Time Savers)

The "System" Shortcut

Treat bodies moving together as one mass to find acceleration quickly.

a = Net Driving Force / Total Mass

Standard Atwood Machine

(Pulley with m₁ > m₂)

  • Acceleration: a = (m₁ - m₂)g / (m₁ + m₂)
  • Tension: T = 2m₁m₂g / (m₁ + m₂)

Constraint Motion Shortcut

For connected pulleys and blocks:

ΣT · a = 0

Trick: (Tension × Accel) for all blocks in a system sums to zero. Use this to find relations between accelerations of different blocks.

Motion on a Rough Incline (Angle θ)

Scenario Resulting Formula
Angle of Repose Block just starts sliding when tan θ = μs
Acc. Sliding Down a = g(sin θ - μkcos θ)
Ret. Sliding Up a = g(sin θ + μkcos θ)

Lami’s Theorem

For exactly 3 coplanar concurrent forces in equilibrium:

F₁/sin α = F₂/sin β = F₃/sin γ

Where the angle in numerator's sine is between the other two forces.

Vertical Circular Motion (VCM)

For a mass tied to a string of length R:

  • Min speed at bottom: umin = √(5gR)
  • Min speed at top: vtop = √(gR)
  • Tension Difference: Tbot - Ttop = 6mg (Always!)

4. Conceptual Insights & Visual Intuition

Pseudo Force (Fp)

A mathematical trick to apply Newton's Laws inside accelerating (non-inertial) frames. Fp = -maframe. Apply it to the mass m in the direction opposite to the frame's acceleration.

Apparent Weight in a Lift

Lift Motion Apparent Weight (R) Feel
Stationary / Const. Vel. mg Normal
Accel. UP ($a$) m(g + a) Heavier
Accel. DOWN ($a$) m(g - a) Lighter
Free Fall ($a=g$) 0 Weightless

Static Friction is "Self-Adjusting"

It is a smart force. If you push a 10 kg block with 5 N and fs,max = 50 N, the friction isn't 50 N. It is exactly 5 N (matching your push to keep a=0).

Centrifugal Force

It is a pseudo force. NEVER draw centrifugal force if you are observing from the ground (inertial frame). Only draw it if you are sitting on the rotating object.

5. Common Mistakes & Traps

⚠️

Trap 1: Assuming N = mg blindly

Normal reaction is NOT always weight.

  • On an incline: N = mg cos θ
  • Pulling upward (F at angle θ): N = mg - F sin θ (This is why pulling is easier than pushing!)
⚠️

Trap 2: Action-Reaction Cancellation

Students think N and mg are action-reaction pairs because they cancel. WRONG. They act on the same body. Action-reaction pairs act on different bodies and never cancel.

⚠️

Trap 3: Cut String vs Cut Spring

  • String: Tension elsewhere changes/vanishes instantaneously.
  • Spring: Force in uncut springs remains unchanged instantaneously (because F=kx, and length cannot change in zero time).

6. Example Applications

Two-Block Friction Problem

Insight: Always assume they move together first. Find common a = F/(mA + mB). Then check the force required to keep the top block accelerating: Freq = mtop × a. If Freq ≤ fs,max, assumption is correct. Else, they slip!

Monkey Climbing a Rope

  • Accelerating UP: T = m(g + a)
  • Accelerating DOWN: T = m(g - a)
  • Falling Freely: T = 0

7. IAT Exam Focus Points

Explosions & Vector Momentum

A bomb at rest explodes into 3 pieces. Two pieces fly off at 90° to each other. Trick: The momentum of the 3rd piece MUST be equal and opposite to the vector sum of the first two pieces: p₃ = -(p₁ + p₂).

Variable Mass (Rocket Propulsion)

Heavy calculus focus. Use Fnet = m(dv/dt) + vrel(dm/dt). Upward thrust on a rocket is Fthrust = vrel(-dm/dt).

Banking of Roads

Condition Safe Speed Formula
Without Friction vopt = √(Rg tan θ)
With Friction (Max) vmax = √[Rg(μs + tan θ) / (1 - μs tan θ)]

Massive Rope Pulling

Finding tension at distance x in a rope of mass M and length L pulled by force F.

Trick: Find a = F/M. Tension at x is mass of remaining part × a:
T(x) = [M/L(L - x)] × a

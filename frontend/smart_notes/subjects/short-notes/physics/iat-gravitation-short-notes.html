1. Core Concept

The Universal Law

Gravitation is a central, conservative, and strictly attractive force. It obeys the Principle of Superposition (vector addition of forces).

The Electrostatics Analogy

Every formula in Gravitation is a mirror image of Electrostatics!

Property Gravitation Electrostatics
Force Constant -G (6.67 × 10-11) k = 1/4πε0 (9 × 109)
Primary Quantity Mass (m) Charge (q)
Potential (V) -GM/r kq/r
Field (E) -GM/r2 kq/r2

Field & Potential

Gravitational Field (Eg or g) is Force per unit mass. Gravitational Potential (V) is Work Done per unit mass.

Eg = -dV/dr
Relation: (Field points in the direction of decreasing potential).

2. Key Formulas

g = GM/R2 = (4/3)πρGR
Acceleration due to Gravity at Surface (where ρ is density).

Variation in g:

Height (h):

Exact: gh = g / (1 + h/R)2.
Approximation (only if h < 5% R): gh ≈ g(1 - 2h/R).

Depth (d):

Exact: gd = g(1 - d/R).
(Decreases linearly to 0 at the center!)

Latitude (λ):

Apparent gravity due to Earth's rotation ω: gλ = g - Rω2cos2λ.
(Max at poles λ=90°, Min at equator λ=0°).

ve = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR)
Escape Velocity (ve). (For Earth, ≈ 11.2 km/s).
vo = √(GM/r)
Orbital Velocity (vo): For a circular orbit at distance r from center.

Crucial Relation

Near the surface (r ≈ R), ve = √2 vo.

3. Elite Shortcuts & Memory Tricks

The "Virial Theorem" Energy Trick (For Circular Orbits):

  • Kinetic Energy K = +GMm/2r
  • Potential Energy U = -GMm/r
  • Total Energy E = -GMm/2r
E = -K = U/2
Master Shortcut: (If you know K is 100 J, you instantly know E = -100 J and U = -200 J!)

Work Done in Lifting a Body:

Work done to lift a mass m from the surface to a height h = nR is exactly:

W = (n / (n+1)) mgR

Example: Lifting a body to height h = R (n=1) &implies; W = mgR/2.
Lifting to h = 2R (n=2) &implies; W = 2/3 mgR.

Neutral Point (Point of Zero Net Force):

If masses m1 and m2 are separated by distance d, the field is zero at a distance x from m1 given by:

x = d / (√(m2/m1) + 1)

5. Conceptual Insights

The Spherical Cavity Trick:

The gravitational field inside a spherical cavity within a uniform solid sphere is completely uniform (constant in magnitude and direction)!
Eg = (4/3)πρG|a| (where a is the vector from the sphere's center to the cavity's center).

Tunnel Through the Earth:

If you drop a ball through a tunnel dug anywhere through the Earth (even a chord, not just the diameter), it executes Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM).

Time Period T = 2π√(R/g) ≈ 84.6 minutes
(Independent of the tunnel's length!)

Field Graphs (Eg vs r):

  • Solid Sphere (Earth): Inside, Eg ∝ r (straight line from origin). Outside, Eg ∝ 1/r2.
  • Hollow Shell: Inside, Eg = 0 (weightlessness everywhere inside!). Outside, Eg ∝ 1/r2.

6. Common Traps & Mistakes

⚠️

The mgh Trap:

Using W = mgh or ΔU = mgh for space/satellites. FATAL ERROR! mgh is valid ONLY for heights h < 10 km. For space, you MUST use the exact formula: ΔU = Uf - Ui = -GMm/rf - (-GMm/ri).

⚠️

The Binomial Approximation Trap:

Using gh = g(1 - 2h/R) when h = R/2. This formula produces garbage if h > 300 km. You must use the exact fraction formula!

⚠️

Escape Velocity Misconceptions:

ve depends ONLY on the mass/radius of the planet and the starting height. It is 100% independent of the mass of the projectile, and independent of the angle of projection!

⚠️

Confusing r with h:

In orbital formulas (U, F, vo, T), r is the distance from the center of the planet. r = Rearth + h. Never plug height h directly into the denominator!

7. Example Applications

Maximum Height of a Projectile:

A particle is fired vertically with speed v = ve/2. Find max height h.

Insight: Apply Energy Conservation at surface and max height:
-GMm/R + (1/2)m(ve/2)2 = -GMm/(R+h) + 0.
Since ve2 = 2GM/R, substituting yields h = R/3.

Satellite Changing Orbits:

Work done to shift a satellite from radius r1 to r2.

Insight: Work Done = Change in TOTAL Energy.
W = Ef - Ei = (-GMm/2r2) - (-GMm/2r1).
Do NOT just subtract Potential Energies (you must account for the change in kinetic energy too).

8. IAT Exam Focus Points

Binary Star Systems (Heavy IAT Favorite)

Two stars of masses m1 and m2 separated by distance d orbit their common center of mass.

Trick: They both revolve with the same angular velocity (ω) and the same Time Period. The mutual gravitational force provides the centripetal force for both:
Gm1m2/d2 = m1ω2r1 = m2ω2r2.

Apogee & Perigee Kinematics:

In an elliptical orbit, angular momentum is strictly conserved about the focus (Sun/Earth).
vmax rmin = vmin rmax.
Velocity is maximum when closest (Perigee/Perihelion) and minimum when farthest (Apogee/Aphelion).

Geostationary vs. Polar Satellites:

Orbit Location Orbits equator Orbits pole-to-pole
Time Period (T) 24 hours (matches Earth's rotation) ≈ 100 minutes

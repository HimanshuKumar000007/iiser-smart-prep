Oscillations (SHM)

1. Core Concept

Motion Classification

Understanding the hierarchy of repetitive motions is crucial for identifying SHM.

Motion Type Condition Example
Periodic Repeats after regular intervals (T) Uniform Circular Motion, Planets
Oscillatory Periodic + To-and-Fro about Mean Pos. Piston in Engine, Swing
SHM Oscillatory + Restoring Force F ∝ -x Spring-Mass, Small Pendulum

Oscillatory Motion

Periodic motion where a body moves back and forth about a mean (equilibrium) position.

Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)

A specific type of oscillation where the restoring force is directly proportional to the displacement and directed towards the mean position: F = -kx.

SHM vs. UCM

SHM is essentially the projection of Uniform Circular Motion (UCM) onto a diameter. If a particle moves in a circle of radius A with angular speed ω, its x-coordinate is x = A cos(ωt + φ), which is the equation for SHM.

2. Key Formulas & Kinematics

F = -mω2x = -kx
Restoring Force Law (ω = √(k/m)).

v(t) = Aω cos(ωt + φ) = ±ω√(A2 - x2)
a(t) = -Aω2 sin(ωt + φ) = -ω2x
Standard SHM Kinematics (A: Amplitude, ω: Angular Frequency, φ: Initial Phase).
T = 2π / ω = 2π √(m/k)
Time Period relationship for Spring-Mass systems.

3. Energy in SHM

Energy Values at Key Points

Position (x) Potential (U) Kinetic (K) Total (E)
Mean (x=0) 0 (1/2)kA2 (1/2)kA2
x = A/2 (1/8)kA2 (3/8)kA2 (1/2)kA2
x = A/√2 (1/4)kA2 (1/4)kA2 (1/2)kA2
Extreme (x=A) (1/2)kA2 0 (1/2)kA2

Strategic Insights

  • Equality Point: At x = A/√2 ≈ 0.707A, the Potential and Kinetic energies are equal (U = K = E/2).
  • Energy Frequency: K and U oscillate at 2f (twice the frequency of displacement), while Total Energy remains constant.

5. Conceptual Insights

Mean vs. Extreme Analysis

  • Mean Position (x=0): v = vmax = Aω, a = 0, U = 0, K = E.
  • Extreme Positions (x=±A): v = 0, a = amax = Aω2, U = E, K = 0.

Superposition of SHM

Resultant of two SHMs in the same direction with same frequency ω is also an SHM with Amplitude Ares = √(A12 + A22 + 2A1A2 cosφ).

6. Common Mistakes & Traps

Pendulum in Accelerated Frames

  • Lift Accel Up (a): geff = g + a &implies; T decreases.
  • Lift Accel Down (a): geff = g - a &implies; T increases.
  • Free Fall: geff = 0 &implies; T = ∞ (No oscillation).

Frequency Double-Check

If Displacement f = fo, then Velocity f = fo and Acceleration f = fo.
But Kinetic and Potential Energy fluctuate at 2fo.

Spring Modification Laws

Spring Constant k ∝ 1/L. If cut into n equal parts, each has constant nk.
Series: 1/keq = Σ(1/ki) | Parallel: keq = Σki.

7. IAT Exam Focus Points

Calculus Check for SHM

Motion is SHM only if d2x/dt2 = -ω2x.
If given a = -2x + 5, the mean position shifts to x = 2.5, but it remains SHM about this new mean.

Phase Relationships

v leads x by π/2.
a leads v by π/2.
a and x are in opposite phase (π).

Spring-Mass Nuances

If the spring has significant mass Ms, the effective time period becomes:
T = 2π √((m + Ms/3) / k).

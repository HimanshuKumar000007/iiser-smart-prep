How to Use IAT PYQs Effectively

  • Solve each paper in timed exam conditions (180 minutes, no breaks)
  • Start with 2021–2025 papers as they reflect the current exam style best
  • After solving, categorize your errors: conceptual mistake, calculation error, or topic gap
  • Identify the topics that repeat across years — these are high-priority
  • For Biology PYQs, cross-reference every answer with your NCERT textbook
  • Track your score improvement across papers: target +5 marks improvement per paper
📊 Repeating Topics in IAT PYQs: Modern Physics (Photoelectric Effect, Atomic Models), Organic Reactions (name reactions), Cell Division (Mitosis vs Meiosis), Genetics (Mendel's Laws), and Calculus (Limits, Integration) appear every year. Prioritize these in your revision.

IAT PYQ vs Mock Tests: What's More Important?

Both are essential, but serve different purposes:

  • PYQs — tell you WHAT the exam tests and WHICH topics repeat
  • Mock tests — build your SPEED, ACCURACY, and TIME MANAGEMENT skills
  • Ideal approach: Use PYQs for pattern recognition, then take fresh mocks to test real performance

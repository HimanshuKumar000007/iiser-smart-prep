IAT Physics rewards conceptual depth over formula memorization. The exam tests your ability to visualize problems, apply first principles, and solve efficiently under time pressure. This ladder—from NCERT to Irodov—builds exactly those skills.

The Physics Mastery Ladder

Don't jump straight to hard problems. Physics requires systematic skill-building. Follow this sequence:

1

NCERT + Exemplar

Build theoretical foundation. Master theory, solved examples, and back exercises.

Step 1: NCERT + Exemplar

NCERT Physics is surprisingly deep. The "Additional Exercises" and "Exemplar Problems" contain IAT-level questions that most students ignore.

Key Insight

Solve every "Additional Exercise" problem. These are deliberately tougher and match IAT's application-based approach. The Exemplar book is a hidden gem—treat it as a separate practice book.

Step 2: HC Verma — The Bible

HC Verma isn't just a book—it's a methodology. The "Concepts of Physics" two-volume set teaches you how to think like a physicist.

📘

How to Use HC Verma

📖

Theory Sections

Read actively. Derive every formula shown. Don't skip the "Wavelength" and "Thought Provoking" boxes.

✏️

Worked Examples

Cover the solution. Attempt first. These 700+ solved problems build your intuition better than any theory.

🎯

Objective I & II

These are IAT-pattern MCQs. Objective II (multiple correct) is especially important for concept testing.

🎯 Vol 1: Mechanics & Thermo
  • Ch 7: Circular Motion
  • Ch 8: Work & Energy
  • Ch 10: Rotational Mechanics
  • Ch 12: Simple Harmonic Motion
  • Ch 23: Heat & Temperature
  • Ch 3: Rest & Motion
  • Ch 5: Newton's Laws
🎯 Vol 2: Elec, Mag & Modern
  • Ch 29: Electric Field & Potential
  • Ch 30: Gauss's Law
  • Ch 34: Magnetic Field
  • Ch 38: Electromagnetic Induction
  • Ch 41: Electric Current
  • Ch 40: Alternating Current
  • Ch 46: Photometry
⚡ Pro Tip: Don't solve all numerical problems. Focus on "Exercises" problems marked with * (star) and all Objective questions. Time is better spent on D.C. Pandey for practice volume.

Step 3: D.C. Pandey — Practice Engine

Once concepts are clear, you need volume. D.C. Pandey's "Understanding Physics" series provides exam-pattern practice with graded difficulty.

📗

D.C. Pandey (Arihant)

Volume-wise Strategy

📚 Priority Volumes
  • Mechanics Vol 1 & 2
  • Electricity & Magnetism
  • Optics & Modern Physics
  • Waves & Thermodynamics
🎯 Section Strategy
  • Introductory Exercise (do all)
  • AIEEE/IIT-JEE MCQs
  • Level 1: Speed building
  • Level 2: Skip initially
⏱️ Time Strategy: Each "Introductory Exercise" should take 30-45 minutes. If you're taking longer, your concepts need revision—go back to HC Verma.

Step 4: Irodov — The Ultimate Test

I.E. Irodov's "Problems in General Physics" is legendary. But be selective—solving all 1800+ problems is overkill and counterproductive for IAT.

  • 1.2: Kinematics

  • 2.1: Electrostatics

  • 1.8: Fluid Mechanics (low weight)
  • 1.9: Relativity (beyond IAT)
  • 2.4: EM Waves (too theoretical)
  • 2.5: Radiation (too advanced)
  • Most of Part 3 & 4
The Irodov Mindset

Each Irodov problem should take 15-20 minutes. If you're stuck for 10 minutes, look at the first step of the solution, then try again. The goal is learning technique, not suffering.

The 8-Week Physics Mastery Plan

1

Weeks 1-2: NCERT Deep Dive

Complete theory + all Additional Exercises. Solve Exemplar problems for starred chapters.

2

Weeks 3-4: HC Verma Concepts

Theory + Worked Examples + Objective I & II. Skip lengthy numerical exercises.

3

Weeks 5-6: D.C. Pandey Volume

Introductory Exercises + AIEEE archives. Build speed: 1 minute per MCQ target.

4

Weeks 7-8: Irodov Selective + Revision

2-3 problems daily from selected chapters. Weekly full mocks. Revise weak areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is HC Verma enough for IISER IAT Physics?

HC Verma builds strong conceptual understanding but additional practice from DC Pandey and PYQs is recommended.

Is Irodov necessary for IISER IAT?

Irodov is optional and mainly useful for advanced students targeting top ranks.

Are NCERT problems important for IISER IAT?

Yes, NCERT and Exemplar problems are extremely important for building the foundation.

1. The Genesis: Why NEST Needed Its Own Dedicated Platform

Over the past few years, thousands of students have trusted IISER SmartPrep to prepare for the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT). However, every single year, hundreds of our students ask the same critical question:

"I am also appearing for the NEST exam for NISER and CEBS. Can I use the same preparation material, or does NEST require a different strategy?"

While the core science concepts in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology share high overlap, the examination DNA of NEST is fundamentally different from IAT:

  • Section-wise Minimum Admissible Score (SMAS): In IAT, there are no individual subject cutoffs — you can compensate a weaker subject with a high score in another. In NEST, you must clear the SMAS cutoff in each of the four subject sections to even qualify for merit ranking.
  • Multi-Correct & Deep Analytical Rigor: NEST questions are crafted by premier DAE scientists at NISER and BARC/TIFR, testing intricate multi-step problem solving and multi-concept synthesis.
  • DAE DISHA Scholarship & Direct BARC Pathway: Admitted students receive ₹60,000 annual stipend plus a ₹20,000 mentorship grant, with direct eligibility to interview for Scientific Officer roles at the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC).

To give NEST aspirants the specialized tools, authentic CBT testing environment, and sectional cutoff simulations they deserve, we built SciPrep.in.

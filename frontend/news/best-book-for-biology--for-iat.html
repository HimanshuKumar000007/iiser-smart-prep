The IAT Biology section tests conceptual clarity, not rote memorization. While NCERT forms the backbone (60-70% questions), Campbell Biology helps you tackle the remaining application-based questions that separate toppers from the rest.

NCERT Biology: The Foundation

NCERT is non-negotiable. Every line, every diagram, every caption is potential question material. The IAT specifically tests your ability to connect concepts across chapters.

📗

NCERT Biology (Class 11 & 12)

NCERT Publications
Must Do 60-70% Weightage

High-Yield Chapters

🧬 Class 11 (Priority)
  • Cell: The Unit of Life
  • Cell Cycle & Cell Division
  • Biomolecules
  • Structural Organisation in Animals
  • Anatomy of Flowering Plants
  • Transport in Plants
  • Photosynthesis in Higher Plants
  • Respiration in Plants
🧬 Class 12 (Priority)
  • Principles of Inheritance & Variation
  • Molecular Basis of Inheritance
  • Human Reproduction
  • Reproductive Health
  • Evolution
  • Human Health & Disease
  • Microbes in Human Welfare
  • Biotechnology: Principles & Processes
💡 Pro Tip: Read the "Summary" at the end of each NCERT chapter first. It gives you the blueprint of what to focus on. Then read the chapter, and finally solve all intext and exercise questions.

How to Study NCERT for IAT

📖

First Reading

Read like a story. Don't highlight. Just understand the flow and key concepts.

🖊️

Second Reading

Active reading. Highlight, make notes, create mind maps for processes.

🔄

Revision Cycles

Revise every 7 days. Use the summary + your notes for quick revision.

Campbell Biology: The Differentiator

Campbell is your weapon for the 30-40% questions that require deeper understanding. Focus only on specific chapters that extend NCERT concepts.

📘

Campbell Biology (12th Edition)

Reece, Urry, Cain et al.
Reference 30-40% Edge

Selective Reading Guide

Don't read cover-to-cover. Use Campbell for these specific topics only:

🔬 Must-Read Units
  • Unit 2: The Cell (Ch 6-12)
  • Unit 3: Genetics (Ch 13-17)
  • Unit 4: Evolution (Ch 22-25)
  • Unit 5: Plant Form & Function (Ch 35-39)
🎯 High-Yield Topics
  • Membrane Transport
  • Cell Signaling
  • DNA Replication & Repair
  • Gene Expression Regulation
  • Photosynthesis mechanisms
  • Respiration pathways
⚠️ Warning: Campbell is 1400+ pages. Reading everything is a trap. Stick to the chapters listed above and use the "Check Your Understanding" questions at chapter ends.

Recommended 6-Week Timeline

1

Weeks 1-2: NCERT Class 11

Focus on Cell Biology, Biomolecules, and Plant Physiology. These form the conceptual base.

2

Weeks 3-4: NCERT Class 12

Genetics, Reproduction, and Biotechnology are high-weightage. Master these first.

3

Weeks 5-6: Campbell Selective

Read only the specified chapters. Focus on understanding mechanisms, not memorization.

Exam Pattern

The IAT 2026 follows a standardized format designed to test your grasp of Class 11 & 12 NCERT concepts across all four subjects.

Feature Details
Duration 3 Hours (180 Minutes)
Questions 60 (15 each in PCMB)
Total Marks 240 Marks
Marking Scheme +4 for Correct, -1 for Incorrect
Mode Computer-Based Test (CBT)
Platform TCS iON

Syllabus Overview

The IAT syllabus is strictly based on Class 11 & 12 NCERT. No need to chase advanced JEE-level concepts—master the basics and their applications.

🧬

Biology

  • Diversity in Living World
  • Cell Structure & Function
  • Genetics & Evolution
  • Human & Plant Physiology
  • Ecology & Environment
  • Biotechnology
🧪

Chemistry

  • Structure of Atom
  • Chemical Bonding & Molecular Structure
  • Thermodynamics
  • Organic Chemistry (Hydrocarbons, Haloalkanes)
  • Coordination Compounds
  • Biomolecules & Polymers
⚛️

Physics

  • Kinematics & Laws of Motion
  • Gravitation & Oscillations
  • Thermodynamics & Kinetic Theory
  • Electrostatics & Current Electricity
  • Magnetic Effects & EMI
  • Optics & Modern Physics
📐

Mathematics

  • Sets, Relations & Functions
  • Calculus (Limits, Derivatives, Integration)
  • Vectors & 3D Geometry
  • Probability & Statistics
  • Matrices & Determinants
  • Complex Numbers & Quadratic Equations

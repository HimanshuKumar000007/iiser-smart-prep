Complete IAT Syllabus 2026: Subject-Wise Guide with High-Yield Topics [PDF]

The IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) 2026 is your gateway to India's premier research institutes. Unlike JEE or NEET, IAT specifically tests your grasp of Class 11-12 NCERT concepts across all four subjects—Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology—with equal weightage. This guide breaks down the complete syllabus, identifies high-yield chapters, and provides a downloadable PDF checklist to track your preparation.

🎯 IAT 2026 Exam Pattern Overview

60 MCQs (15 per subject) • 180 minutes • +4/-1 marking • Computer-based (TCS iON platform) • NCERT Class 11 & 12 syllabus

Before diving into subject-wise details, understand this: IAT questions are conceptual, not calculative. They test understanding over formula manipulation. A question might blend thermodynamics with kinetic theory or genetics with evolution—something JEE/NEET rarely does.

📐 Physics Syllabus for IAT 2026

Class 11 Topics

Chapter Key Topics Priority
Physical World & Measurement Units, dimensions, significant figures, error analysis Medium
Kinematics Motion in 1D/2D, relative velocity, projectile motion High
Laws of Motion Newton's laws, friction, circular motion, banking of roads High
Work, Energy & Power Work-energy theorem, conservative forces, collisions High
System of Particles Center of mass, momentum conservation, rotational motion High
Gravitation Kepler's laws, orbital velocity, escape velocity, gravitational potential energy High
Properties of Bulk Matter Elasticity, viscosity, surface tension, capillarity Medium
Thermodynamics Laws of thermodynamics, processes (isothermal, adiabatic), efficiency of heat engines Critical
Kinetic Theory Ideal gas equation, degrees of freedom, mean free path Medium
Oscillations SHM, energy in oscillations, time period of simple pendulum Medium
Waves Superposition principle, standing waves, beats, Doppler effect High

Class 12 Topics

Chapter Key Topics Priority
Electrostatics Coulomb's law, Gauss's law, electric potential, capacitors Critical
Current Electricity Kirchhoff's laws, Wheatstone bridge, potentiometer, meter bridge Critical
Magnetism Biot-Savart law, Ampere's law, torque on magnetic dipole, moving coil galvanometer High
EMI & AC Faraday's law, Lenz's law, self and mutual inductance, LCR circuits, resonance High
EM Waves Displacement current, electromagnetic spectrum, properties of EM waves Medium

Semiconductors PN junction, diodes, transistors as amplifiers, logic gates Medium
Communication Systems Modulation, demodulation, bandwidth of signals, propagation of EM waves Low
🎯 Physics: High-Yield Focus Areas (PYQ Analysis 2017-2025)
  1. Mechanics (25-30%): Rotation and conservation laws appear every single year
  2. Electromagnetism (25%): Electrostatics and current electricity are non-negotiable
  3. Modern Physics (15-20%): Direct formula-based, highest scoring per minute invested
  4. Thermodynamics (10-15%): Often combined with kinetic theory in single questions

IAT-Specific Tip: Questions on error analysis and significant figures appear frequently but are ignored by JEE/NEET aspirants. Don't skip the first chapter.

States of Matter Gas laws, kinetic theory of gases, deviation from ideal behavior, van der Waals equation Medium
Thermodynamics Enthalpy, Hess's law, Gibbs free energy, spontaneity criteria Critical

Organic Chemistry Basics IUPAC nomenclature, isomerism, reaction mechanisms (inductive, resonance, hyperconjugation) High
Hydrocarbons Conformations of ethane and butane, aromaticity, reactions of alkanes, alkenes, alkynes Medium

Chapter Key Topics Priority

Chemical Kinetics Rate laws, order and molecularity, Arrhenius equation, collision theory, catalysis Critical
Surface Chemistry Adsorption (physisorption, chemisorption), colloids, emulsions Medium
General Principles of Metallurgy Concentration of ores, extraction processes, Ellingham diagram Low
d and f Block Elements Properties, oxidation states, lanthanoid contraction, colored ions Medium
Coordination Compounds IUPAC nomenclature, Werner's theory, VBT, CFT, isomerism, color and magnetism High

Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers Preparation methods, reactions, acidic nature of phenols, Williamson synthesis High
Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids Nucleophilic addition reactions, aldol condensation, Cannizzaro reaction, Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky reaction Critical
Amines Basicity, preparation, reactions with nitrous acid, diazonium salts Medium
Biomolecules Carbohydrates (glucose, fructose), proteins (structure, enzymes), nucleic acids (DNA, RNA) High
Polymers Types of polymers, polymerization methods (addition, condensation) Low
Chemistry in Everyday Life Drugs and their classification, food preservatives, cleansing agents Low
🎯 Chemistry: Weightage Distribution
  • Physical Chemistry (35-40%): Numericals from thermodynamics, equilibrium, electrochemistry
  • Organic Chemistry (30-35%): Mechanism-based questions dominate; name reactions are gold
  • Inorganic Chemistry (25-30%): Chemical bonding and coordination compounds are must-know

IAT-Specific Tip: Biomolecules is surprisingly high-weightage in IAT compared to JEE. Expect questions on enzyme action and DNA structure rather than just definitions.

📊 Mathematics Syllabus for IAT 2026

Class 11 Topics

Chapter Key Topics Priority
Sets Operations on sets, Venn diagrams, power set, practical problems Low
Relations and Functions Types of relations, functions (one-one, onto, inverse), composition of functions, graphs High
Trigonometric Functions Identities, equations, properties of triangles, sine and cosine formulae, inverse trigonometric functions (basics) Critical
Complex Numbers Algebraic operations, Argand plane, modulus and argument, polar form, cube roots of unity High
Linear Inequalities Algebraic and graphical solution of linear inequalities in one and two variables Low
Permutations and Combinations Fundamental principle of counting, nPr, nCr, circular permutations, restrictions High
Binomial Theorem General and middle terms, properties of binomial coefficients, approximation High
Sequences and Series AP, GP, HP, arithmetic-geometric series, sum to n terms, method of differences High
Straight Lines Various forms of equation of a line, angle between lines, distance of a point from a line Critical
Conic Sections Circle, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola - standard equations, properties, parametric forms Critical
Introduction to 3D Geometry Coordinate axes, coordinate planes, distance between points, section formula Medium
Limits and Derivatives Intuitive idea of limit, standard limits, derivative as rate of change, differentiation of polynomials and trigonometric functions Critical
Statistics Measures of dispersion (mean deviation, variance, standard deviation), correlation Medium
Probability Random experiments, events, axiomatic probability, addition and multiplication theorems Medium

Class 12 Topics

Chapter Key Topics Priority
Matrices Types, operations, transpose, symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices, inverse of a matrix by elementary operations Critical
Determinants Properties, minors and cofactors, adjoint and inverse, solving system of linear equations Critical
Continuity and Differentiability Continuity, differentiability, exponential and logarithmic functions, logarithmic differentiation, Rolle's and Mean Value Theorems High
Application of Derivatives Increasing/decreasing functions, tangents and normals, maxima and minima, rate of change Critical
Integrals Integration by substitution, partial fractions, parts, definite integrals, fundamental theorem of calculus Critical
Application of Integrals Area under simple curves, area between two curves High
Differential Equations Order and degree, formation, variable separable, homogeneous, linear differential equations High
Vector Algebra Direction cosines, addition, scalar and vector products, scalar triple product Critical
Three Dimensional Geometry Direction cosines and ratios, equation of line and plane, angle between lines and planes, distance of a point from a plane High
Linear Programming Introduction, graphical method of solution, types of LPP Medium
Probability Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, random variables, probability distribution, Bernoulli trials, Binomial distribution High
🎯 Mathematics: Chapter-Wise Weightage
  1. Calculus (40-45%): Limits, differentiation, integration, differential equations—forms the backbone
  2. Algebra (25-30%): Complex numbers, P&C, binomial, matrices—conceptual and scoring
  3. Coordinate Geometry (20-25%): Conic sections are heavily tested with standard results
  4. Trigonometry (10-15%): Equations and inverse trigonometric functions

IAT-Specific Tip: Vector algebra questions often combine with 3D geometry. IAT loves probability questions involving Bayes' theorem—more than JEE Main does.

Environmental Issues Air, water and soil pollution, radioactive waste, greenhouse effect, ozone depletion, deforestation Medium
🎯 Biology: Most Scoring Areas
  • Human Physiology (25-30%): NCERT diagrams are directly tested—know every label
  • Genetics & Evolution (20-25%): Pedigree analysis, DNA replication mechanisms
  • Cell Biology (15-20%): Cell division stages, enzyme action, cell organelle functions
  • Ecology (15-20%): Food webs, pyramids, succession—easy marks if NCERT is thorough

IAT-Specific Tip: Biotechnology questions focus on processes (PCR steps, recombinant DNA technique) rather than just applications. Know the steps, not just definitions.

🔗 Cross-Subject Integration: The IAT Difference

Unlike board exams, IAT questions often blend concepts from multiple subjects:

Question Type Subjects Involved Example
Thermodynamics Physics + Chemistry Comparing efficiency of heat engines and chemical reactions
Kinetics Physics + Chemistry Collision theory applied to gas molecules and reaction rates
Genetics Biology + Math Probability calculations in inheritance patterns (dihybrid cross)
Optics Physics + Math Calculus in wave interference and diffraction patterns

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the IAT syllabus and preparation.

Is the IAT syllabus exactly NCERT?
95% yes. The vast majority of questions come directly from NCERT line-by-line. The remaining 5% test application of NCERT concepts in unfamiliar contexts. Don't study beyond NCERT for theory, but practice application through IAT-specific PYQs.
Do I need to study Class 11 if I'm currently in Class 12?
Absolutely. IAT has equal weightage for Class 11 and 12 (30 questions each). Many students lose marks by ignoring 11th-grade revision. Our 3-Month Planner includes dedicated Class 11 revision weeks.
Are diagrams important in Biology?
Critical. IAT often asks questions based on NCERT diagrams—labeling parts, identifying structures, or explaining processes shown in figures. Focus especially on: cell organelles, photosynthesis apparatus, human heart/brain, and genetic crosses.
Which subject is most scoring in IAT?
Depends on your background:
  • Biology for PCB students (direct NCERT recall, least calculation)
  • Mathematics for PCM students (objective marking, no ambiguity)
  • Physics and Chemistry require equal effort for both groups
The key is balancing all four—IISERs look for consistent scores, not subject toppers.
How is IAT different from NEST or JEE?
IAT NCERT-focused, 4 subjects, moderate difficulty, speed matters
NEST Slightly beyond NCERT, 5 sections including general, section-wise cutoff
JEE Calculation-heavy, requires advanced problem-solving, single/double correct
Can I prepare for IAT alongside JEE/NEET?
Yes, with adjustments. Your JEE/NEET prep covers 80% of IAT. The differences:
  • IAT has Biology for all (PCM students must prepare)
  • IAT questions are faster to solve but cover more topics superficially
  • IAT has no integer type—all MCQs with +4/-1 marking
Add 4-5 full IAT mocks in the final month to adapt to the pattern.

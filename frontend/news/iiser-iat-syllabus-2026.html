IISER IAT 2026 Syllabus Overview

The IISER IAT 2026 syllabus is based on NCERT Class 11 and 12 curriculum. The exam has 60 MCQs across 4 subjects — each contributing 15 questions and 45 marks (+3 correct, −1 incorrect).

Is NCERT Enough for IAT 2026?

✅ Biology: NCERT Class 11 & 12 covers approximately 85–90% of IAT Biology questions. Read every line including boxes and diagrams.
✅ Chemistry: NCERT covers approximately 70–75% of IAT Chemistry. Focus on inorganic reactions, organic mechanisms, and environmental chemistry.
⚠️ Physics & Mathematics: NCERT provides the conceptual base (~60–65%), but you need IAT previous year papers and assertion-reason practice for complete preparation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IISER IAT 2026 syllabus?+
IISER IAT 2026 syllabus covers Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology from NCERT Class 11 and 12. Each subject has 15 questions. Key topics include Modern Physics, Organic Chemistry, Calculus, Genetics, and Cell Biology.
Is NCERT enough for IISER IAT 2026?+
NCERT covers 65–90% of IAT depending on subject. Biology (~85%), Chemistry (~75%), Physics & Math (~65%). Supplement with IAT previous year papers for complete preparation.
Does IAT 2026 include Biology?+
Yes. Biology is mandatory — 15 questions, 45 marks (25% of the paper). Covers Cell Biology, Genetics, Physiology, Ecology from NCERT Class 11–12. This is the major difference from JEE Main.

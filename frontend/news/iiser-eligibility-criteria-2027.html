IISER Eligibility Criteria 2027: Qualifying Marks, Stream Rules (PCB vs PCM), Age Limit & Attempts

📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱️ 9 Min Read ✍️ By IISER SmartPrep Research Team 🎯 Valid for IAT 2027 Aspirants

Are you a pure Biology student wondering if you can get into an Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) without Class 12 Mathematics? Or a JEE aspirant wanting to know if Biology is compulsory? With over 1,40,000+ candidates registering for the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) annually, understanding the official eligibility guidelines set by the Joint Admissions Committee (JAC) is your crucial first step.

In this comprehensive guide, we unpack every parameter of the IISER Eligibility Criteria 2027: required Class 12 board marks, stream regulations (PCB, PCM, PCMB), age limits, attempt policies, reservation documents, and channel guidelines.

⚡ Quick Eligibility Snapshot at a Glance (IAT 2027)

  • Admissions Channel: 100% through the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT). KVPY and JEE Advanced channels are retired.
  • Qualifying Examination: Class 12 or equivalent with science stream from any recognized central/state board.
  • Minimum Percentage: 60% marks in aggregate for General, EWS, and OBC-NCL; 55% marks for SC, ST, and PwD.
  • Stream Eligibility: Both pure PCB and pure PCM students are 100% eligible. Mathematics is NOT mandatory to apply!
  • Passing Years Allowed: Candidates who passed Class 12 in 2025, 2026, or are appearing in 2027.
  • Upper Age Limit: No age restriction exists for IAT.

1. Class 12 Board Percentage & Minimum Qualifying Marks

To be eligible for counseling and seat allotment across any of the 7 IISERs (Pune, Kolkata, Mohali, Bhopal, Thiruvananthapuram, Tirupati, Berhampur), candidates must satisfy the aggregate percentage benchmarks prescribed in their qualifying Class 12 (or equivalent) board examination.

Candidate Category Minimum Aggregate Marks in Class 12 Criteria Type Verification Mechanism
General / Unreserved (UR) 60% or 6.0/10 CGPA Aggregate across all subjects Official Board Marksheet
Economically Weaker Section (GEN-EWS) 60% or 6.0/10 CGPA Aggregate across all subjects Valid EWS Certificate + Marksheet
Other Backward Classes (OBC-NCL) 60% or 6.0/10 CGPA Non-Creamy Layer Central List Central OBC-NCL Certificate + Marksheet
Scheduled Caste (SC) 55% or 5.5/10 CGPA Aggregate across all subjects Category Caste Certificate
Scheduled Tribe (ST) 55% or 5.5/10 CGPA Aggregate across all subjects Category Tribe Certificate
Persons with Disabilities (PwD) 55% or 5.5/10 CGPA Disability ≥ 40% (UDID card) Govt Medical Board Certificate

⚠️ Calculation Rule for Aggregate Marks

Aggregate marks are calculated based on all subjects listed on your official marksheet. If your board evaluates best-of-5 or best-of-4, the total aggregate reflected in the final board passing certificate is counted. Rounding off (e.g. 59.8% rounded to 60%) is NOT permitted by the Joint Admissions Committee.

2. Stream Eligibility: Can PCB and PCM Students Apply?

This is by far the most frequent query from NEET and JEE aspirants. Let us clear the confusion once and for all:

A. Pure PCB Students (Physics, Chemistry, Biology • Without Math)

YES! PCB students are 100% eligible. Mathematics in Class 12 is NOT a prerequisite for appearing in the IISER Aptitude Test or for getting admission to the 5-Year BS-MS dual degree program.

  • The IAT exam paper has 4 sections: Physics (15 Qs), Chemistry (15 Qs), Biology (15 Qs), and Mathematics (15 Qs).
  • There are NO sectional cutoffs in IAT. Your All India Rank (AIR) is determined purely by your total score out of 240.
  • PCB students regularly score 140–165+ marks simply by securing 42–48 in Biology, 40–45 in Chemistry, 35–42 in Physics, and solving 3–5 basic high-yield questions in Mathematics (like Sets, Statistics, or Mathematical Reasoning).

B. Pure PCM Students (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics • Without Biology)

YES! PCM students are also 100% eligible. You do not need to have taken Biology in your Class 12 board exams.

  • JEE Main and Advanced aspirants have a strong quantitative grip on Math and Physics numericals.
  • By learning basic NCERT 11th and 12th Biology chapters (such as Cell Biology, Ecology, and Genetics), PCM students frequently score an extra 20–28 marks in Biology with minimal effort!

C. PCMB Students (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology)

PCMB students enjoy a distinct natural advantage because they have familiarity with all 4 subjects. They can attempt questions across the entire 60-question paper and comfortably hit 170+ marks for IISER Pune or IISER Kolkata.

3. Age Limit & Number of Allowed Attempts for IAT 2027

Unlike many competitive entrance tests that enforce stringent age restrictions, IISER guidelines prioritize academic timeline over birth dates:

  • Upper Age Limit: There is NO upper age limit prescribed for the IISER Aptitude Test.
  • Passing Year Restriction: You must have cleared your Class 12 board examination in 2025 or 2026, or you must be appearing for your Class 12 exams in 2027.
  • Number of Attempts: Effectively, a student can attempt IAT a maximum of three times (in their Class 12 appearing year, first dropper year, and second dropper year). Candidates who passed Class 12 in 2024 or earlier are ineligible for IAT 2027.

4. Single Admissions Channel: Why IAT is Now the Only Route

Historically, IISER admissions occurred through three distinct streams:

  1. SCB Channel (State & Central Boards): Writing the IAT exam.
  2. KVPY Channel: Based on Kishore Vaigyanik Protsahan Yojana fellowship ranks.
  3. JEE Advanced Channel: Within top 10,000 ranks in JEE Advanced.

Key Update for 2026–2027: The Joint Admissions Committee has streamlined admissions into a single unified portal. 100% of seats across all 7 IISERs are allocated exclusively via the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT). Neither JEE Advanced ranks nor KVPY scores are accepted for IISER allocations.

💡 Pro-Tip on Certificate Validity Dates

Both OBC-NCL and GEN-EWS certificates must be issued on or after April 1, 2026 for the 2026–2027 admission cycle. State-level caste certificates that do not correspond to the Central OBC list are automatically rejected during document scrutiny. Apply early to avoid last-minute portal rejections!

6. Partner Institutes Accepting IAT 2027 Scores

A major advantage of writing IAT is that your single examination score opens the gateway to multiple premier research institutions across India beyond the 7 IISERs:

  • IISc Bengaluru (Indian Institute of Science): Admits students to its four-year Bachelor of Science (Research) program through IAT.
  • IIT Madras: Uses IAT scores for admission to its pioneering BS in Medical Sciences & Engineering.
  • 7 IISER Campuses: IISER Pune, IISER Kolkata, IISER Mohali, IISER Bhopal, IISER Thiruvananthapuram, IISER Tirupati, and IISER Berhampur.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can PCB students get admission without Mathematics in Class 12?
Yes! PCB students are 100% eligible for the 5-Year BS-MS dual degree program across all IISERs. Mathematics is not mandatory in Class 12. There are no sectional cutoffs in IAT, meaning your score in Bio, Chem, and Physics counts toward your overall rank.
Is there negative marking in the IISER Aptitude Test?
Yes. The exam has 60 questions totaling 240 marks. Each correct answer carries +4 marks, while an incorrect answer results in a penalty of -1 mark. Unanswered questions receive 0 marks.
What if I have 59.9% in Class 12 as a General candidate?
Unfortunately, you will not meet the eligibility cutoff. The Joint Admissions Committee strictly mandates a minimum of 60.0% aggregate marks for General/OBC/EWS candidates without any round-off allowance.
Are double droppers eligible for IISER IAT 2027?
Yes, provided you passed your Class 12 board examination in 2025. Candidates who passed in 2025, 2026, or are appearing in 2027 are eligible for the 2027 examination cycle.
Can diploma holders or NIOS students appear for IAT?
Students from the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) who have taken science subjects (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology) with the required minimum aggregate marks are fully eligible. Three-year polytechnic diploma holders are not eligible for IAT BS-MS admissions.

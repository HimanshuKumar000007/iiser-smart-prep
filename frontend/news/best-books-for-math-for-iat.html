IAT Mathematics tests five distinct skill sets: Algebraic manipulation, Calculus operations, Geometric visualization, Trigonometric identities, and Vector analysis. Unlike JEE Advanced, IAT Math focuses on accuracy over speed, with problems testing conceptual clarity rather than complex calculations.

The Five Pillars of IAT Mathematics

IAT Mathematics requires balanced preparation across Algebra, Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, and Vectors & 3D Geometry. Neglect any pillar and risk losing 15-20 marks.

Algebra — The Foundation of Problem Solving

Algebra forms the backbone of IAT Mathematics, covering 30-35% of the math section. It tests your ability to manipulate expressions, solve equations, and understand abstract structures. RD Sharma provides the comprehensive coverage needed for IAT level.

∑

The RD Sharma Method

📖

Theory First

Read theory sections thoroughly. Pay attention to solved examples showing step-by-step manipulation techniques.

✏️

Level 1 & 2

Complete all Level 1 (Basic) and Level 2 (Moderate) questions. Skip Level 3 initially — return after mocks.

🎯

MCQ Focus

IAT is MCQ-based. Practice objective questions specifically. Time yourself: 2.5 minutes per problem.

High-Yield Algebraic Identities
(a + b + c)² = a² + b² + c² + 2(ab + bc + ca) a³ + b³ + c³ - 3abc = (a + b + c)(a² + b² + c² - ab - bc - ca)

Master these identities. IAT frequently tests their application in simplifying complex expressions and solving equations.

Higher Algebra

Hall & Knight
Advanced Theory Deep

Use Hall & Knight only when RD Sharma's theory feels insufficient, especially for Theory of Equations and Advanced Combinatorics. This book is classic but extensive — focus only on chapters matching IAT syllabus. Skip outdated content and overly complex proofs.

Calculus — The Mathematics of Change

Calculus is the highest-weighted section in IAT Mathematics (25-30%). It separates serious aspirants from casual ones. IA Maron provides the perfect balance of theory and graded problems for IAT preparation.

The Maron Approach

📚

Theoretical Preliminaries

Don't skip theory sections. Maron explains limits and continuity with rigorous intuition — crucial for IAT conceptual questions.

🔢

Problem Sets I & II

Set I builds fundamentals. Set II mirrors IAT difficulty. Complete both before moving to advanced problems.

📈

Application Problems

Focus on maxima/minima and rate of change problems. IAT loves real-world application questions.

Differentiation & Integration Shortcuts
d/dx[ln|x|] = 1/x    ∫(1/x)dx = ln|x| + C ∫eˣ[f(x) + f'(x)]dx = eˣf(x) + C

The second formula (eˣ integration trick) appears in 60% of IAT integration problems. Recognize the pattern: eˣ multiplied by (function + its derivative).

Speed Tip: For limit problems, always check for 0/0 or ∞/∞ form first. If found, apply L'Hospital's Rule immediately — saves 2 minutes per problem compared to algebraic manipulation.

Cengage Calculus

G. Tewani
JEE Advanced Extra Practice

Use Cengage only if you've completed Maron and need more practice. Cengage has excellent solved examples but problems are JEE Advanced level — harder than IAT. Focus on "Single Correct Answer" type questions only. Skip "Multiple Correct" and "Integer Type" initially.

Coordinate Geometry & Trigonometry — The Visual Branches

Coordinate Geometry and Trigonometry together comprise 35-40% of IAT Math. These topics reward visualization and formula recall. SL Loney's classic texts provide the rigorous foundation needed for IAT's geometry problems.

Conic Section Essentials
Parabola: y² = 4ax    Focus: (a,0)    Directrix: x = -a Ellipse: x²/a² + y²/b² = 1    e = √(1 - b²/a²)

For conic sections, always identify the standard form first. IAT rarely tests shifted conics — focus on origin-centered forms and their properties.

📐
Visualization Hack: For Circle problems, use the "geometric properties" approach (angle in semicircle = 90°, etc.) before coordinate bash. Often saves 3-4 minutes and reduces calculation errors.
🧮

NCERT + RD Sharma (Vectors & 3D)

NCERT Class 12 + RD Sharma
Sufficient IAT Level

For Vectors and 3D Geometry, NCERT theory + RD Sharma problems are sufficient for IAT. These topics are formula-heavy — create flashcards for direction cosines, plane equations, and line equations. IAT tests standard problems, not complex 3D visualizations. Focus on: scalar/vector triple products, plane equations, and angle between lines/planes.

The 10-Week Math Mastery Plan

Mathematics topics are interconnected. Study Algebra and Calculus in parallel to build cross-topic problem-solving skills.

Weeks 1-2

Algebra Foundation

RD Sharma: Complex Numbers, Quadratic Equations, Progressions. Daily: 15 problems + formula revision. Build algebraic manipulation speed.

Weeks 2-3

Calculus: Limits & Differentiation

IA Maron: Limits, Continuity, Differentiation basics. Focus on conceptual understanding of limits. Daily: 10 problems.

Weeks 3-4

Coordinate Geometry: Lines & Circles

SL Loney Part I: Straight Lines and Circles. Master all formulas. Daily: 10 problems + diagram practice.

Weeks 5-6

Algebra: Binomial & Probability

RD Sharma: Binomial Theorem, P&C, Probability. These are high-scoring chapters. Daily: 15 problems.

Weeks 6-7

Calculus: Integration & Applications

IA Maron: Indefinite and Definite Integration. Master standard integrals. Daily: 12 problems.

Weeks 8-9

Conic Sections & Trigonometry

SL Loney: Parabola, Ellipse, Hyperbola + Trigonometry identities. Formula intensive phase. Daily: 15 problems.

Week 10

Vectors, 3D & Full Revision

NCERT + RD Sharma: Vectors and 3D Geometry. Then revise all formulas. Take 2 full-length math sectional tests.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which book is best for IISER IAT Mathematics?

RD Sharma for algebra, IA Maron for calculus, and SL Loney for coordinate geometry are among the best books for IISER IAT mathematics preparation.

Is RD Sharma enough for IISER IAT maths?

RD Sharma covers most algebra topics required for IAT, but students should also practice calculus from IA Maron and geometry from SL Loney.

How many math questions come in IISER IAT?

The IISER Aptitude Test usually includes around 15 mathematics questions covering algebra, calculus, trigonometry, and geometry.

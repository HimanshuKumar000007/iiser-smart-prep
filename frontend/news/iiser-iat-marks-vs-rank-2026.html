IISER IAT Marks vs Rank 2026: Expected Cutoffs, Category & Campus-Wise Safe Scores

📅 August 25, 2026 ⏱️ 12 min read ✍️ By IISER SmartPrep Academic Research Team

The IISER Aptitude Test (IAT) is the sole admission channel for securing seats in the prestigious 5-year BS-MS Dual Degree and 4-year BS programs across the seven IISER campuses (Pune, Kolkata, Mohali, Bhopal, Thiruvananthapuram, Tirupati, and Berhampur), alongside dual-degree admission at IISc Bangalore and IIT Madras (BS Medical Sciences & Engineering).

With over 1,40,000+ candidates expected to compete in 2026 for approximately 2,100+ seats, understanding the Marks vs Rank trend is critical to calibrating your study strategy, setting realistic sectional targets, and locking in your dream campus.

📌 IAT 2026 Quick Pattern Overview

Total Marks: 240 Marks • Total Questions: 60 Questions (15 Physics, 15 Chemistry, 15 Mathematics, 15 Biology) • Marking Scheme: +4 for Correct, -1 for Incorrect • Sectional Cutoffs: NONE (Overall total aggregate only).

4. The 4-Subject Formula: How Non-PCMB Students Can Break 150+ Marks

A common misconception among aspirants from pure PCM (Engineering track) or pure PCB (Medical track) backgrounds is that they cannot compete with PCMB students. In reality, over 65% of selected IISER candidates are non-PCMB students who executed a smart 3+1 subject plan:

💡 The 3+1 Scoring Blueprint

3 Master Subjects (Your Core): Aim for 40 to 48 marks out of 60 in each (10 to 12 correct questions with minimal negative marks) → Yields 120 to 135 marks.
1 High-Yield 4th Subject: Select 4 to 6 basic NCERT direct questions (no calculus required for PCB in Math; basic genetics/ecology/biomolecules for PCM in Bio) → Yields 16 to 24 marks.
Combined Score: 136 to 159+ Marks (Instantly locks a Top-4 IISER seat!).

High-Yield 4th Subject Chapters to Target:

  • For PCM Students attempting Biology: Cell Structure & Division, Biomolecules (overlaps with Chemistry!), Ecology & Environment, Genetics fundamentals, Photosynthesis basics.
  • For PCB Students attempting Mathematics: Matrices & Determinants, Statistics, Mathematical Reasoning, Straight Lines, Sequence & Series, Probability basics.

5. Counseling Rounds & Float / Slide Dynamics

IISER Joint Admissions Committee (JAC) conducts 6 to 8 rounds of centralized online counseling:

  1. Round 1 & 2: Cutoffs remain strict as top JEE/NEET rankers hold provisional seats.
  2. Round 3 & 4: Substantial drops in closing ranks occur as medical (NEET) and engineering (JoSAA/IIT) counseling sessions finalize, causing candidates to vacate provisional IISER seats.
  3. Round 5+ & Spot Rounds: Ranks jump significantly for newer campuses like Tirupati and Berhampur. Never surrender your seat early if you select the Float option!

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What score is needed for IISER Pune in IAT 2026? ▼
For General category candidates, a score of 165–175+ marks out of 240 is recommended to comfortably secure IISER Pune in the initial rounds.
Are there negative marks in IISER IAT? ▼
Yes. Each correct question awards +4 marks, while every incorrect attempt incurs a penalty of -1 mark. Unattempted questions carry 0 marks.
Is 120 marks a good score in IAT? ▼
Yes! A score of 120/240 corresponds to approximately AIR 2,800–3,500, which has historically been sufficient to secure admission to IISER Bhopal, IISER Thiruvananthapuram, IISER Tirupati, or IISER Berhampur.
Does IAT require sectional cutoffs in each subject? ▼
No. There are no subject-wise or sectional cutoffs in the IISER Aptitude Test. Your overall aggregate total across all 60 questions determines your All India Rank.
Can I also get admission to NISER through IAT? ▼
No. NISER Bhubaneswar and UM-DAE CEBS Mumbai admit students exclusively through the NEST exam. To prepare dedicatedly for NEST, visit our partner portal at SciPrep.in.

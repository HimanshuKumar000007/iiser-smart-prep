IISER IAT Preparation Strategy 2026: Month-by-Month Plan

The complete preparation roadmap for IISER IAT 2026 — with subject-wise tips, best books, and a month-by-month study plan designed to maximize your score.

Month-by-Month IAT 2026 Study Plan

Whether you're starting fresh or already preparing for JEE, this timeline ensures optimal coverage for IAT 2026 (exam typically held in June).

January – February 2026: Foundation Phase

Complete NCERT for Physics (Class 11 & 12). Begin NCERT Biology systematically — Cell Biology, Genetics first. Revise Algebra & Trigonometry from Mathematics. Target: 4–5 hours/day.

March 2026: Concept Strengthening

Complete NCERT Chemistry (Physical + Inorganic). Finish remaining Biology chapters. Start solving IAT Previous Year Papers (2017–2020). Identify your weak topics. Take 1 full mock test/week.

April 2026: Speed & Accuracy Phase

Intensive PYQ practice (2021–2025). Focus on Organic Chemistry reactions and Modern Physics questions. Take 2 full mocks/week. Analyze every mistake. Start Biology revision (high-yield topics only).

May 2026: Revision & Consolidation

Full syllabus rapid revision. 3 mocks/week. Focus on assertion-reason question type. Revise all NCERT diagrams and examples. Draft a formula sheet for quick last-minute review.

June 2026: Final Sprint

Light revision only. Solve 1 full mock 3 days before exam. Focus on Biology high-yield chapters and important reactions in Chemistry. Stay calm — conceptual clarity matters most in IAT. Exam: ~June 15, 2026

Subject-Wise Preparation Strategy

🔬 Physics Strategy

Focus on conceptual understanding over calculations. IAT tests "why" not "how many". Practice assertion-reason questions from Modern Physics, Electrostatics, and Optics. H.C. Verma's conceptual questions are excellent for IAT-level depth.

⚗️ Chemistry Strategy

NCERT is the backbone. Memorise organic reaction mechanisms and name reactions. For Inorganic, create concept maps for P-Block and D-Block. Biomolecules and Environmental Chemistry are scoring and relatively easy sections.

📐 Mathematics Strategy

IAT Math is moderate — NCERT + R.D. Sharma exercises cover it well. Prioritize Calculus (Limits, Derivatives, Integration), Probability, and Vectors. Unlike JEE, very complex multi-step problems are rare in IAT.

🌱 Biology Strategy

NCERT Class 11 & 12 is sufficient for 85%+ of Biology questions. Read every line including boxes, bold text, and diagrams. Cell Biology, Genetics, and Human Physiology have the highest weightage. Create one-page cheat sheets per chapter.

Chemistry NCERT Class 11 & 12 O.P. Tandon (Organic), J.D. Lee (Inorganic)

Key Tips to Crack IAT 2026

  • Solve all IAT previous year papers (2017–2025) — the most important resource
  • Practice in TCS iON interface to get comfortable with the actual exam environment
  • Don't skip Biology — it can be a game-changer (15 easy NCERT-based questions)
  • Maintain an error log — track every mistake and revisit weekly
  • Allocate time per section: 45 min Physics, 45 min Chemistry, 45 min Math, 45 min Biology
  • Attempt all questions — the -1 penalty is manageable with educated guessing
💡 Smart Tip: Students who also write JEE Main should start dedicated IAT Biology preparation at least 3 months before IAT. The PCM preparation overlaps well, but Biology requires dedicated time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many months needed to prepare for IISER IAT?+
For JEE students, 2–3 months of targeted Biology + IAT-style question practice is sufficient. For fresh starters, 6–8 months of consistent NCERT-based preparation is recommended.
How to prepare for IISER IAT without coaching?+
Self-study works very well for IAT. Use NCERT textbooks for all subjects, solve IAT previous year papers (2017–2025), take regular full-length mock tests, and track your weak areas. Many IAT toppers are self-taught.
What books are best for IISER IAT 2026?+
Physics: NCERT + H.C. Verma. Chemistry: NCERT + O.P. Tandon Organic. Mathematics: NCERT + R.D. Sharma. Biology: NCERT Class 11–12 is the primary and most important resource. Trueman's Biology is useful for extra MCQ practice.

IAT Chemistry tests three distinct skills: Mechanistic thinking (Organic), factual recall with pattern recognition (Inorganic), and mathematical problem-solving (Physical). Each branch needs a different book and strategy — master all three for a top score.

The Three Branches of Chemistry

IAT Chemistry is equally distributed across Organic, Inorganic, and Physical. Neglect any one branch and you lose 33% of your marks.

Organic Chemistry — The Mechanism Game

Organic Chemistry is not about memorizing reactions. It's about understanding electron flow, identifying nucleophiles and electrophiles, and predicting products from first principles.

🧪

Advanced Organic Chemistry

M.S. Chauhan (Balaji Publications)
Most Important Mechanism Focus

How to Use MS Chauhan

📖

Level 1: Theory

Read mechanism boxes carefully. Draw every curved arrow. Understand WHY, not just WHAT.

✏️

Level 2: Solved Ex

Cover solution. Predict product. Check mechanism. Build intuition for electron movement.

🎯

Level 3: Practice

Do all MCQs. Time yourself: 2 minutes per problem. Review mistakes with mechanism.

High-Yield Reaction Map
Alkene Reactions → Alcohols & Ethers → Carbonyl Chemistry → Carboxylic Acids → Amines & Biomolecules
🎯 Priority Chapters
  • GOC (General Organic Chem)
  • Hydrocarbons
  • Alkyl Halides
  • Alcohols, Phenols, Ethers
  • Aldehydes & Ketones
  • Carboxylic Acids
  • Amines
  • Biomolecules
⚡ Mechanism Masters
  • SN1 vs SN2 (always tested)
  • E1 vs E2 elimination
  • Nucleophilic addition
  • Aldol condensation
  • Cannizzaro reaction
  • Hoffmann elimination
  • Reimer-Tiemann
  • Friedel-Crafts
⚗️ Pro Tip: Create a "Mechanism Notebook." Draw every mechanism you encounter without looking at the solution. If you can't draw the curved arrows, you don't understand the reaction.
📗

Organic Chemistry (Morrison & Boyd)

Robert Morrison & Robert Boyd
Reference Deep Theory

Use Morrison & Boyd only when MS Chauhan's explanation feels insufficient. Read Chapter 1 (Structure) and Chapter 6 (Stereochemistry) completely — these build foundational intuition. Skip problem-solving; use only for conceptual clarity.

Inorganic Chemistry — The Memory + Pattern Game

Inorganic seems like pure memorization, but IAT tests patterns: periodic trends, coordination chemistry logic, and chemical bonding principles. VK Jaiswal teaches you to predict, not memorize.

⚛️

Inorganic Chemistry

V.K. Jaiswal (Balaji Publications)
Essential Pattern Based

The Jaiswal Method

📊

Trend Tables

Don't memorize values. Understand trends: IE, EA, EN, atomic radius. Draw the patterns.

🔷

Coordination Logic

VBT, CFT, and color — master the logic of splitting and pairing energy. Predict geometry.

📝

NCERT Integration

Jaiswal + NCERT together. Jaiswal explains; NCERT provides the exact IAT-level facts.

🎯 High-Weightage
  • Chemical Bonding
  • Coordination Compounds
  • p-Block Elements
  • d & f Block Elements
  • Metallurgy
  • Environmental Chemistry
📋 Must Memorize
  • Color of compounds
  • Magnetic moments
  • Oxidation states
  • Reactivity series
  • Flame test colors
  • Important ores
Coordination Chemistry Formula

CFSE = (-0.4 × n(t2g) + 0.6 × n(eg)) × Δo + (pairing energy corrections)

Master this calculation. IAT always tests whether you understand splitting in octahedral vs tetrahedral fields.

⚛️ Memory Hack: Create "color cards" — flashcards with compound on one side, color/magnetic property on other. Review daily for 10 minutes. Spaced repetition beats cramming.

Physical Chemistry — The Math Branch

Physical Chemistry is 40% conceptual understanding and 60% problem-solving speed. N Awasthi provides the graded practice needed to solve IAT-level problems in under 90 seconds.

📘

Physical Chemistry

Narendra Awasthi (Balaji Publications)
Problem Solving Graded Levels

The Level System

Awasthi's book is organized in three levels. Don't skip levels — each builds calculation speed and conceptual depth.

🥉

Level 1: Basic

Formula application. Build accuracy. Target: 3 min/problem. Do ALL problems.

🥈

Level 2: JEE Main

IAT-level difficulty. Multi-concept problems. Target: 2 min/problem.

🥇

Level 3: Advanced

Skip initially. Return after mocks. For 160+ scorers only.

🎯 Priority Chapters
  • Mole Concept
  • Atomic Structure
  • Gaseous State
  • Thermodynamics
  • Chemical Equilibrium
  • Ionic Equilibrium
  • Electrochemistry
  • Chemical Kinetics
⚡ Calculation Tips
  • Use approximation
  • Master log tables
  • Unit conversion speed
  • Significant figures
  • Graph interpretations
  • Dimensional analysis
🧮 Speed Tip: Physical Chemistry rewards approximation. If options are far apart (factor of 2 or more), approximate aggressively. Save exact calculation for close options only.
📙

NCERT Physical Chemistry

NCERT Class 11 & 12
Theory Base Numericals

NCERT Physical Chemistry is surprisingly problem-rich. Solve every "Intext Question" and "Exercise" numerical. The "Additional Exercises" contain IAT-level problems that most students skip. Don't be most students.

The 10-Week Integrated Plan

Chemistry branches are interconnected. Study them in parallel, not sequentially, to build cross-branch intuition.

Weeks 1-3

Organic Foundation + GOC

MS Chauhan Level 1 (GOC, Hydrocarbons). Build mechanism intuition. Daily: 5 problems + 2 mechanism drawings.

Weeks 2-4

Inorganic: Bonding + Periodicity

VK Jaiswal Chemical Bonding + NCERT Periodicity. Create trend tables. Daily: 20 facts review.

Weeks 3-5

Physical: Mole + Gaseous + Thermo

N Awasthi Level 1-2. Focus on calculation speed. Daily: 10 problems with timer.

Weeks 6-7

Organic: Carbonyl + Carboxylic

MS Chauhan Level 2. Master nucleophilic addition. Connect to Biomolecules.

Weeks 7-8

Inorganic: Coordination + p-Block

VK Jaiswal + NCERT. Color compounds, VBT, CFT. Daily flashcard review.

Weeks 8-10

Physical: Equilibrium + Electrochem

N Awasthi Level 2 + Previous Year IAT problems. Mock test integration.

How to Use Subject-Wise Mocks Strategically

  • Start by taking one mock per subject to identify your baseline accuracy in each
  • Prioritize subjects below 60% accuracy — fix these first, they offer the most rank improvement
  • For Biology (IAT-exclusive), JEE students must start fresh — use NCERT + our chapter mocks
  • In Mathematics, practice Calculus and Coordinate Geometry aggressively — highest IAT weight
  • For Physics, focus on Mechanics, Optics, and Modern Physics which appear every year
  • In Chemistry, Organic Chemistry questions are application-based — practice mechanisms, not memorization
  • After subject mastery, move to full-length mocks to practice time allocation across all 4 subjects
💡 Pro Strategy: The fastest rank improvement comes from Biology — it's untouched by JEE aspirants. Scoring 40+ out of 45 in Biology (easily achievable with 2–3 weeks NCERT practice) can push your rank from 600 to under 200.

IISER IAT Subject-Wise Cutoff Benchmarks

To secure admission at top IISERs (Pune, Kolkata), aim for a total score above 120/180. Here are subject-wise benchmarks to target in your mock tests:

⚛️ Physics — Target: 30–36/45

Physics in IAT tests conceptual clarity rather than calculation speed. Questions often come from Assertion-Reason format. If you score below 24/45 consistently in mocks, focus on Mechanics and Modern Physics chapter mocks first.

🧪 Chemistry — Target: 33–39/45

Chemistry is the most scoring subject if you're systematic. Organic Chemistry (named reactions, isomerism, IUPAC nomenclature) tends to dominate with 6–8 questions per paper. Physical Chemistry problems are calculation-based and straightforward.

📐 Mathematics — Target: 27–36/45

IAT Math is the toughest section. Calculus alone contributes 4–5 questions. If Maths is your weak spot, use our chapter-level mocks to isolate topics. Probability and Coordinate Geometry are high-yield with manageable difficulty.

🧬 Biology — Target: 36–42/45

Biology is your biggest opportunity. The NCERT Class 11 and 12 Biology books are 100% sufficient. Focus on: Genetics (Mendel's Laws, inheritance), Ecology (ecosystems, biodiversity), Human Physiology, and Cell Biology. Consistent NCERT reading + our Biology mocks = 40+ guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there subject-wise mock tests for IISER IAT?+
Yes. IISER Smart Prep offers dedicated subject-wise mock tests for all four IAT subjects — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology. Each set has 15–30 questions modeled on real IAT difficulty with +3/-1 marking.
Which is the hardest subject in IISER IAT?+
Mathematics is statistically the most difficult subject in IISER IAT, with average student scores typically 5–10 marks lower than other subjects. Biology is the easiest for students who prepare systematically using NCERT, as it has no JEE overlap and is concept-based.
How many questions per subject in IISER IAT 2026?+
Each subject (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology) has 15 questions in IAT, making 60 questions in total. The marking scheme is +3 for correct, -1 for incorrect, and 0 for unattempted. Total maximum score is 180.
Do JEE students need Biology mock tests for IAT?+
Absolutely. Biology accounts for 25% of the IAT paper (15 out of 60 questions, 45 out of 180 marks) and has zero overlap with JEE. JEE aspirants who skip Biology preparation typically score 10–20 marks in the Biology section, losing significant rank positions. Dedicated Biology mock tests are essential.
How should I use subject-wise mocks in my IAT study plan?+
In Month 1, take one subject mock per subject to identify weak areas. In Month 2, do intensive topic-level mock drills for each weak subject. In Month 3, transition to full-length 180-minute mocks to practice time management across all subjects.

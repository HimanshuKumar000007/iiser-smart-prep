IISER Seat Matrix 2027: Campus-Wise Seats, Category Reservation & Total Undergraduate Intake

📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱️ 10 Min Read ✍️ By IISER SmartPrep Admissions Desk 🏛️ Covers All 7 IISERs + IISc + IITM

Understanding the exact IISER Seat Matrix is the cornerstone of a winning counseling and preference-filling strategy. Knowing how many seats exist at each campus—and how they are divided across General, EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, and PwD categories—enables you to gauge your realistic chances of landing your dream campus like IISER Pune or Kolkata, even before the first allotment round begins.

For the 2026–2027 academic session, the Joint Admissions Committee (JAC) will allocate over 2,050+ seats across the 7 IISERs, alongside specialized seat pools at IISc Bengaluru and IIT Madras via the IISER Aptitude Test (IAT). Here is the complete, official seat distribution and counseling roadmap.

📌 Key Seat Matrix Takeaways at a Glance

  • Total IISER BS-MS Seats: Over 2,050+ seats across all 7 Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research.
  • Largest Science Intake: IISER Thiruvananthapuram (TVM) with 320 seats, followed by IISER Pune with 288 seats.
  • Engineering & Economics Quota: IISER Bhopal offers 115 4-Year BS Engineering Sciences seats and 43 4-Year BS Economic Sciences seats.
  • External Partner Seats: IISc Bengaluru (BS Research) and IIT Madras (BS Medical Sciences) allocate seats through IAT.
  • Central Reservation: Standard Central Government quotas apply (40.5% General, 10% EWS, 27% OBC-NCL, 15% SC, 7.5% ST, and 5% horizontal PwD).

4. Strategic Counseling Mastery: Freeze vs Float Rules

Once the IAT result is declared, counseling occurs over 6 to 8 consecutive online rounds. Knowing how to manipulate the Float vs Freeze options is crucial to securing an upgrade to a top-tier campus:

💡 How "Float" Helps You Upgrade to Pune or Kolkata

Suppose you rank your preferences as: 1. Pune, 2. Kolkata, 3. Mohali, 4. Bhopal. In Round 1, if you are allotted IISER Mohali, you must pay the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF: ₹35,000 for Gen/OBC; ₹17,500 for SC/ST) to confirm your admission. Choose the FLOAT option! In Round 2 or Round 3, if seats open up in Kolkata or Pune due to withdrawals, the JAC algorithm will automatically upgrade you without requiring any extra payment!

Key Rules of the Seat Acceptance Fee (SAF):

  1. If you receive an offer in any round and fail to pay the SAF before the deadline, you are permanently eliminated from all subsequent counseling rounds.
  2. The SAF amount is not an extra charge—it gets directly adjusted against your 1st semester academic fees when you report to campus.
  3. If you decide to withdraw before the official national withdrawal cutoff, your SAF is refunded after a nominal processing charge (typically ₹1,000).

5. Recommended Campus Preference Order for IAT Aspirants

While every student has unique geographical and research preferences, the historical consensus preference order among top rankers is:

  1. IISER Pune: Typically closes earliest (General AIR 600–850). Best known for biology, interdisciplinary sciences, and international research tie-ups.
  2. IISER Kolkata: Renowned for astrophysics (CESSI), earth sciences, and synthetic chemistry (Closes around General AIR 1,200–1,600).
  3. IISER Mohali: Celebrated for quantum physics, mathematics, and high international PhD placement rate (Closes around General AIR 2,200–2,600).
  4. IISER Bhopal: Ideal for students interested in 4-year Engineering Sciences (EECS/Data Science) and economics alongside natural sciences (Closes around General AIR 3,000–3,600).
  5. IISER Thiruvananthapuram (TVM): Lush campus with top-tier physics, chemistry, and environmental science labs (Closes around General AIR 3,600–4,200).
  6. IISER Tirupati & Berhampur: Rapidly expanding permanent campuses with modern equipment and smaller, focused cohorts (Closes around General AIR 4,400–5,200).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can a PCB student choose IISER Bhopal Engineering Sciences?
No. Admission to the 4-Year BS Engineering Sciences and BS Economic Sciences programs at IISER Bhopal strictly mandates that the candidate must have studied Mathematics in Class 12. However, PCB students are fully eligible for the 5-Year BS-MS Natural Sciences dual degree at Bhopal and all other IISERs.
Are female-only supernumerary seats available at IISERs?
Unlike IITs (which enforce a 20% female supernumerary quota), IISERs currently do not have a dedicated female supernumerary reservation. Female candidates compete directly within the General, EWS, OBC, SC, ST, and PwD categories based on merit.
What happens if I get a seat in Round 1 and choose Freeze?
Choosing 'Freeze' permanently locks your allotted campus and program. You will NOT be considered for any further upgrades in subsequent rounds, and your seat at that campus is finalized.
Is there a spot round in IISER counseling?
Yes, if vacant seats remain after the regular 6–8 counseling rounds, the Joint Admissions Committee occasionally conducts an additional post-counseling mop-up or spot round for registered candidates who haven't secured a seat.

How to Use IAT Mock Tests Effectively

  • Take a full mock in exam conditions — no phone, no breaks, timed exactly 3 hours
  • After the test, spend at least 1 hour analyzing every wrong and skipped question
  • Maintain a score tracker — aim for a steady improvement of 5–10 marks per mock
  • Take topic-wise mocks for subjects where your accuracy is below 60%
  • Practice time allocation: ~45 minutes per subject is the ideal target
  • Aim for at least 10–15 full mocks before the actual exam
🎯 Target Score Guide: For general category admission to top IISERs (Pune, Kolkata), aim for 120+ out of 180 in your mock tests. Scores of 100–120 are competitive for other campuses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there free IISER IAT mock tests?+
Yes. IISER Smart Prep offers free full-length IAT mock tests in the exact TCS iON interface. Topic-wise mocks for Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology are also available free at iisersmartprep.com.
How many mock tests should I take for IAT?+
Ideally, attempt at least 10–15 full-length mock tests before IAT. Focus on quality analysis after each mock — identify weak areas, improve accuracy, and track your improvement. Supplement with topic-wise mocks for low-scoring subjects.
How is IAT mock test different from JEE mock tests?+
IAT mocks have 60 questions (15 each in PCMB) with +3/-1 marking vs JEE's 90 questions in PCM with +4/-1. IAT mock questions are more conceptual and include assertion-reason questions. IAT also has a Biology section which JEE does not.

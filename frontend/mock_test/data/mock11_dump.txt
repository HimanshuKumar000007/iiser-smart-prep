--- Q1 [Physics] ---
Q: A particle moves in a circle of radius R with constant speed v. The magnitude of average velocity in half revolution is:
Options: ['2v/π', 'v', '2v/πR', 'πv/2']
Correct: 0
Explanation: <strong>Step 1: Displacement</strong><br>Displacement in half revolution = 2R (diameter).<br><strong>Step 2: Time Taken</strong><br>Time = πR/v (half of time period 2πR/v).<br><strong>Step 3: Average Velocity</strong><br>Average velocity = Displacement / Time = 2R / (πR/v) = 2v/π.

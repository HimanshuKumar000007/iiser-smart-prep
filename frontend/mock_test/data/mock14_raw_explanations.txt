### Question 1
**Difficulty:** Medium | **Time:** 3 minutes  
**Topic:** Mechanics - Kinematics  
**Answer:** D (Corrected from A)

**Solution:**

Given: x = 3t³ - 9t² + 15t + 5
The particle comes to rest when velocity v = 0
Step 1: Find velocity by differentiating position
v = dx/dt = 9t² - 18t + 15
Step 2: Set v = 0
3t² - 6t + 5 = 0
Step 3: Calculate discriminant
Δ = b² - 4ac = (-6)² - 4(3)(5) = 36 - 60 = -24
Since discriminant is negative, there are no real roots, meaning the particle never comes to rest.

### Question 2 [IMAGE-BASED]
**Difficulty:** Medium | **Time:** 3 minutes  
**Topic:** Electrostatics  
**Answer:** C

**Solution:**

Given: Four charges at square corners, side length a
- Top-left: +Q
- Top-right: -Q
- Bottom-left: -Q
- Bottom-right: +Q

Step 1: Distance from corner to center
d = a/√2
Step 2: Electric field due to one charge at center
E₁ = kQ/d² = kQ/(a²/2) = 2kQ/a²
Step 3: Analyze field directions
Fields from opposite charges on diagonal cancel in one direction, while others add.
Net field magnitude:
E_net = 2 × E₁ × √2 = 2 × (2kQ/a²) × √2 = 2√2 kQ/a²

### Question 5 [IMAGE-BASED]

**Topic:** Optics - Ray Diagrams  
**Answer:** A

**Solution:**

Given: Convex lens with focal length f
Object distance u = -3f
Step 1: Use lens formula
1/f = 1/v - 1/u
1/f = 1/v - 1/(-3f)
1/f = 1/v + 1/(3f)
Step 2: Solve for v
1/v = 1/f - 1/(3f) = 2/(3f)
v = 3f/2
Step 3: Calculate magnification
m = v/u = (3f/2)/(-3f) = -1/2

**Answer:** C

**Solution:**

Given:
- Source frequency f₀ = 500 Hz
- Source velocity vₛ = 30 m/s (toward observer)
- Sound velocity v = 330 m/s
- Observer stationary (vₒ = 0)

Step 1: Use Doppler formula (source moving toward stationary observer)
f' = f₀ × [v/(v - vₛ)]
Step 2: Substitute values
f' = 500 × [330/(330 - 30)] = 500 × [330/300] = 500 × 1.1 = 550 Hz

### Question 7
**Difficulty:** Hard | **Time:** 5 minutes  
**Topic:** Electromagnetism  
**Answer:** B

**Solution:**

Given: Two identical coils, radius R, current I, separated by distance R
For a circular coil, magnetic field at distance x along axis:
B = (μ₀IR²)/(2(R² + x²)^(3/2))
Step 1: Field at midpoint due to each coil
Distance from each coil to midpoint = R/2; x = R/2
Step 2: Field due to one coil
B₁ = (μ₀IR²)/(2(R² + R²/4)^(3/2)) = (4μ₀I)/(5√5 R)
Step 3: Total field (both coils contribute equally)
B_total = 2B₁ = 2 × (4μ₀I)/(5√5 R) = (8μ₀I)/(5√5 R)

### Question 13
**Difficulty:** Medium | **Time:** 3 minutes  
**Topic:** Capacitance - Multi-concept  
**Answer:** B

**Solution:**

Given: Two identical capacitors, capacitance C each, in series, charged to V
Step 1: In series, charge on each
Q = C_eq × V where C_eq = C/2 → Q = CV/2
Step 2: When reconnected in parallel (same polarity)
Total charge = 2Q = 2(CV/2) = CV
Total capacitance = 2C
Step 3: Voltage across parallel combination
V' = Total charge/Total capacitance = CV/(2C) = V/2

Q5 [S: Physics]: A long straight wire carries current I and passes near a small current loop of area A oriented such that loop plane is perpendicular to wire at distance d. Torque on loop (small loop approximation) is proportional to:
Options: ['I²A/d', 'I A / d²', 'I A / d', 'I² A / d²']
Correct: 1
Explanation: <b>Step 1: Magnetic Field of Wire</b><br>Field B at distance d from an infinite wire carrying current I is B = (μ₀I) / (2πd).<br><br><b>Step 2: Magnetic Moment of Loop</b><br>Magnetic moment m = I_loop × Area = I × A (assuming same current I).<br><br><b>Step 3: Torque Calculation</b><br>Torque τ = m × B.<br>Magnitude τ = (IA) × (μ₀I / 2πd) ∝ I²A/d.

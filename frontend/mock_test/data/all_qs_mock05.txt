--- Q1 [Physics] ---
Q: A ball is dropped from a height of 80 m. After striking the ground, it rebounds to a height of 20 m. If the time of contact with the ground is 0.2 s, what is the average acceleration during contact? (Take g = 10 m/s²)
Options: ['100 m/s² upward', '200 m/s² upward', '300 m/s² upward', '400 m/s² upward']
Correct Index: 2 -> |300 m/s² upward| 
Explanation: <strong>Step 1: Calculate Velocity Just Before Impact</strong><br>Using v² = u² + 2gh (dropped u=0):<br>v_down = √(2 × 10 × 80) = √1600 = 40 m/s (downward).<br><strong>Step 2: Calculate Velocity Just After Impact</strong><br>Using v² = u² - 2gh (reaches max height 20m, v=0 at top):<br>u_up = √(2 × 10 × 20) = √400 = 20 m/s (upward).<br><strong>Step 3: Calculate Change in Velocity</strong><br>Take upward as positive.<br>Δv = v_final - v_initial = (+20) - (-40) = 60 m/s.<br><strong>Step 4: Calculate Average Acceleration</strong><br>a = Δv / Δt = 60 / 0.2 = 300 m/s².<br>Direction is positive (upward).

Q: **Passage:** A container has two compartments separated by a movable partition. Initially, compartment A contains 2 moles of a diatomic ideal gas at 300 K and compartment B contains 3 moles of the same gas at 400 K. The partition is then removed and the system reaches thermal equilibrium.

What is the final temperature of the gas mixture?
Options: ['340 K', '350 K', '360 K', '370 K']
Correct Index: 2 -> |360 K| 
Explanation: <strong>Step 1: Energy Conservation</strong><br>Since it's an ideal gas mixing, total internal energy is conserved.<br>U = n C_v T.<br>U_total = U_A + U_B.<br>(n_A + n_B) C_v T_final = n_A C_v T_A + n_B C_v T_B.<br><strong>Step 2: Solve for T_final</strong><br>T_final = (n_A T_A + n_B T_B) / (n_A + n_B).<br>T_final = (2×300 + 3×400) / (2+3) = (600 + 1200) / 5 = 1800 / 5 = 360 K.

## 2. Exam Strategy for Students

### A. Section Order Strategy
- **Start with your strongest subject** (usually Biology or Chemistry) to build confidence and secure quick marks.
- **Middle Phase:** Attack your second strongest subject. Keep momentum high.
- **Late Phase:** Tackle calculation-heavy sections (Physics and Math).
- **Recommended Order (Example for a PCMB student):** Biology -> Chemistry -> Physics -> Mathematics.
  - *Note:* If you are a PCM student, do not skip Biology entirely! Attempt the easy/conceptual Bio questions, as IISER questions can sometimes be derived from general 10th-grade reasoning or basic 11th NCERT.

### B. Time Management (180 Minutes)
- **Biology (15 Qs):** 20 - 25 minutes. Read carefully, don't rush the tricky 'except' or 'not' questions.
- **Chemistry (15 Qs):** 35 - 40 minutes. Prioritize Inorganic & Organic first; save Physical Chem calculations for later.
- **Physics (15 Qs):** 50 - 55 minutes. If a numerical takes >4 minutes, mark for review and move on.
- **Mathematics (15 Qs):** 50 - 55 minutes. Focus on algebra and coordinate geometry early; tackle intense calculus at the end.
- **Buffer/Review Time:** Remaining 5-15 minutes to revisit marked questions or double-check OMR/digital selections.

### C. Guessing Strategy
- **IAT has Negative Marking (-1 for incorrect).** Blind guessing is strictly prohibited as it destroys rank.
- **Intelligent Elimination:** Only guess if you can confidently eliminate 2 out of the 4 options (a 50-50 guess). Statistically, a 50-50 guess across multiple questions yields a net positive expected value (+4 for correct, -1 for incorrect gives an EV of +1.5 per question).
- **Never guess on \"Hard\" numericals** unless you have a strong dimensional analysis backing your intuition.

## 3. Estimated Cut-Off Marks (Target 2024-2026 Analysis)

*Note: The IAT is out of 240 marks.*

- **Top 1% (Rank < 300 - Target IISER Pune/Kolkata):**
  - **Estimated Score:** 155 - 170+
  - **Reasoning:** Requires near-perfect accuracy in two subjects and strong performance (>60%) in the remaining two. High scorers will capitalize on the medium-difficulty questions and avoid silly mistakes on the easy ones.

- **Top 10% (Rank 3000 - Safe for most IISERs):**
  - **Estimated Score:** 110 - 125
  - **Reasoning:** achievable by securing solid marks in 3 subjects (approx 35-40 per subject) and selectively attempting the 4th subject. Avoiding negative marking is the biggest differentiator here.

- **Average Candidate (Rank 10000+):**
  - **Estimated Score:** 65 - 80
  - **Reasoning:** Average candidates usually attempt only 2-3 subjects and suffer heavily from negative marking by guessing on hard questions.

**Final Advice:** Consistency is your greatest weapon. Treat this Mock Test like the real exam, sit for 3 continuous hours without distraction, and spend at least 2 hours analyzing your mistakes afterward!

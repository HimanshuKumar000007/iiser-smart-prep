Why Practice IISER IAT Mock Tests?

IISER IAT mock tests simulate the real TCS iON exam environment, helping students improve time management and build exam temperament. The IAT is a 180-minute, 60-question paper covering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology — each section carrying 15 questions. Practicing full-length and subject-wise mock tests regularly helps identify weak topics, reduce silly mistakes, and improve overall speed and accuracy. IISER IAT Syllabus 2026 – What to Study

The IISER IAT 2026 syllabus is based on the NCERT Class 11 and Class 12 curriculum across four subjects. In Physics, key topics include Mechanics, Electrostatics, Modern Physics, Waves, and Optics. In Chemistry, focus areas are Organic Chemistry (particularly named reactions and isomerism), Electrochemistry, and Chemical Bonding. In Mathematics, Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Probability, and Vectors are high-yield. In Biology, topics from Genetics, Ecology, Human Physiology, and Cell Biology appear most frequently in the IAT paper.

IISER IAT Preparation Strategy 2026

The most effective IISER IAT preparation strategy involves a structured 3-month plan: Month 1 — complete NCERT revision and solve chapter-wise topic mocks; Month 2 — attempt 2–3 full-length mocks per week and analyze performance using AI analytics; Month 3 — intensive PYQ practice, targeted weak-topic revision, and daily quick mocks for speed. Students combined with our AI Doubt Solver, which answers any PCMB question instantly, achieve significantly better results compared to traditional coaching center approaches.

IISER IAT vs JEE – Key Differences

While both the IISER IAT and JEE Main test PCMB concepts, the IAT has a distinctly different emphasis. The IAT Biology section (absent in JEE) accounts for 25% of the paper, and questions lean more conceptual and application-based rather than formula-heavy. Students who are strong in NCERT Biology and conceptual Physics often find the IAT more accessible than JEE Advanced. For JEE aspirants who also appear for IAT, focusing on the Biology section and practicing IAT-specific mock tests is the key differentiator.

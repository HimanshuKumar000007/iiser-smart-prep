IISER College Predictor 2026

The IISER College Predictor helps students estimate their chances of admission into IISER institutes based on their IAT score. By analyzing previous year cutoff trends and seat matrix, this predictor estimates your expected rank and possible institutes.

How IISER Rank is Calculated

The predicted rank is calculated using historical IAT marks vs rank data from previous years including IISER 2024 and 2025 admissions. Higher marks correspond to lower (better) ranks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good score in IISER IAT?

A score above 170 in IISER IAT usually corresponds to a rank within the top 500, securing admission in top IISERs.

How accurate is the IISER college predictor?

The predictor estimates rank using previous year IAT marks vs rank trends. It uses statistical models and AI-based rank analysis to give the most accurate range possible.

What rank is required for IISER Pune?

For the general category, a rank under 1000 is typically considered safe for IISER Pune, requiring a score of 160 or higher.

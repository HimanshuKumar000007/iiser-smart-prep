Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how IISER Smart Prep collects, uses, and protects your data.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the data necessary to provide you with a personalized learning experience:

  • Identity: Email address for account management.
  • Security: Login credentials (stored with industry-standard encryption).
  • Transactions: Payment status (e.g., "Paid" or "Unpaid"). We do not store card details.
  • Academics: Mock test scores, time spent per question, and analytics data.

2. Payment Security

All financial transactions are processed through Razorpay, a secure third-party payment gateway.

We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card numbers, UPI IDs, or net banking passwords.

3. How We Use Data

  • To provide and evaluate your mock tests.
  • To generate personalized performance analytics.
  • To unlock premium features and maintain subscription status.
  • To improve platform performance.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage and session cookies solely for authentication (keeping you logged in) and session management.

5. Data Sharing

We value your trust. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties.

6. Data Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information when you enter, submit, or access your personal information.

7. User Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. To do so, please contact our support team.

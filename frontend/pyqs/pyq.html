IAT 2024 Physics • Rotational Dynamics
Q: A uniform solid sphere of mass M and radius R rolls without slipping down an inclined plane of inclination θ. What is the linear acceleration of the center of mass of the sphere?

Detailed AI Solution Logic:

For a rolling body without slipping, linear acceleration is given by a = (g sin θ) / (1 + I/MR²). For a solid sphere, I = (2/5) MR², hence a = (5/7) g sin θ.

What is IISER IAT Exam?

The IISER IAT (Integrated Aptitude Test) is the national-level entrance exam conducted by IISERs for admission to BS-MS Dual Degree programmes. The exam consists of 60 multiple-choice questions spread equally across four subjects — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology (15 questions each). The marking scheme awards +3 marks for every correct answer and deducts −1 mark for incorrect ones, with a total duration of 3 hours and a maximum score of 180 marks. Solving IISER IAT previous year papers is one of the most reliable ways to understand what the real exam looks and feels like.

Why Solving IISER PYQs is Important

Practising IISER PYQ papers is essential for every serious aspirant. First, they help you understand the exact exam pattern — the type of questions, difficulty distribution, and subject weightage that repeats year after year. Second, they reveal the most important topics in each subject so you can prioritise your revision. Third, timed practice on real IAT PYQ papers dramatically improves your speed and accuracy under exam conditions. The more past papers you solve, the more confident and exam-ready you become.
